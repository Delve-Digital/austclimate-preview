2a:T409,<p>As ducted heating works by drawing in air, your ductwork will naturally fill up with dust, dirt or debris. While the system will have filters, even those components can become clogged if they are not properly cleaned.</p>
<p>Although a build-up of dust won’t necessarily lead to a full system failure, it can still affect the overall performance and efficiency. This typically causes the ducted system to work harder to heat the required area, which not only translates to higher running costs but also shortens its overall lifespan.</p>
<p>This is why cleaning is a major part of any quality gas ducted heating service. An experienced heating and cooling technician will get into the difficult areas, clear out your ductwork and make sure everything is in tip-top shape.</p>
<p>A regular service will both prevent any serious issues and allow you to save money by avoiding expensive repairs. <h2>What are some of the signs that a service is required?</h2>
<p>Beyond the recommended 2-3 year guidelines, there are a number of signs that every gas heater owner should keep an eye out for. If you notice any of the following issues, don't hesitate to contact your local licensed HVAC company.</p>
<p><br/></p>
<ul>
<ul><li>Unusual smells</li>
<li>A build-up of dirt or debris</li>
<li>A faulty pilot light</li>
<li>A yellow flame (take immediate action)</li>
<li>Higher than average energy consumption<br/></li>
</ul>
<p>These are just some of the common problems that may occur. If something seems to be out of the ordinary, it is always a good idea to play it safe and schedule a service. Remember, gas appliances require the expertise of a licensed professional. </p>
<h2>I own an old gas heater. Should I get it inspected?</h2>
<p>Yes. In recent years, more and more information has surfaced about the dangers of carbon monoxide spreading in homes. Both open-flue heaters and older ducted systems have been shown to produce unsafe carbon monoxide levels. This may lead to anything from a flu-like illness to serious and potentially deadly consequences.</p><p>Therefore, it is highly recommended that you organise an inspection from a licensed gas technician.</p>
<p>Even if there isn’t an issue, this could be an excellent opportunity to upgrade and invest in a newer more efficient system. Whether it’s a mounted split system or ducted heating, there are endless options available that do not have the same risks as your old gas heater.</p>
