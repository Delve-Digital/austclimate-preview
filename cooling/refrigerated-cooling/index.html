What Is Refrigerated Cooling?

Refrigerated heating and cooling systems work by drawing in the warm air from your indoor environment. The refrigerant within the unit absorbs the heat from the air, and the cool, fresh air is circulated back into your home.

The flexible nature of refrigerated cooling and heating systems has made them a popular choice in both residential and commercial spaces.

Benefits of refrigerated cooling systems include:

Refrigerated Cooling

  • Not Reliant on Outdoor Conditions: As they don’t rely on outside air, refrigerated cooling systems will still work in humid and rainy conditions.
  • Precise Temperature Control: Refrigerated cooling systems let you set the ideal temperature for your space, regardless of the weather outside.
  • Year-Round Comfort: Many refrigerated cooling systems are built with reverse-cycle technology, so you can heat as well as cool your space.
  • Range of Options: Depending on your needs and the design of your property, you can opt for refrigerated cooling in the form of a split system or ducted air conditioning.
  • Energy Efficiency: Many modern systems are designed with advanced technology to optimise energy use, reducing your power bill and environmental impact in one.
  • Sleek, Unobtrusive Designs: With a range of options available, it’s easy to find a model that blends seamlessly with your home’s design.
  • Advanced Features: Quality refrigerated cooling systems can include advanced features, like WiFi control and easy-to-use timers, for added convenience.

Add-on Cooling Systems

Add-on or dual cycle cooling is designed to be attached to a new or existing gas ducted heating system. This gives you year-round comfort throughout your home or business. This fully integrated air conditioning system provides cool, filtered and dehumidified air through the same ductwork and grilles as your gas ducted heating system.
