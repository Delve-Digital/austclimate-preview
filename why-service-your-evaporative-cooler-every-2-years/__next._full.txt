24:T55e,<p>Evaporative cooling is one of the cleanest, most efficient ways to keep your home cool and comfortable. To ensure your cooler is always operating at its best, it's important to invest in regularly scheduled servicing.</p>
<p>Some technicians may recommend annual servicing, while others simply ask you to follow manufacturer instructions. At Australian Climate Systems, we strongly recommend servicing your evaporative cooler every 2-3 years.</p>
<p><strong><em>But why are regular </em></strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/services/services-and-maintenance/"><strong><em>evaporative cooling services</em></strong></a><strong><em> important?</em></strong> Here are just a few benefits of regular servicing for your evaporative cooler:</p>
<ul><li>Reduced breakdowns and less need for urgent repairs</li><li>Less energy consumed and fewer greenhouse gases emitted</li><li>A longer-lasting cooling unit that doesn't need to be replaced prematurely</li><li>Fresher air and more consistent cooling performance</li><li>Peace of mind for you and your family</li></ul>
<p></p>
<p><strong><em>Read on for more information about servicing your evaporative cooler. </em></strong>We'll cover the <em>benefits of regular servicing</em>, the <em>problems that a cooler service can solve</em>, the <em>cost of evaporative cooling services</em> and repairs, and much more.</p>
<p></p>25:T5a5,<p>Evaporative cooling produces naturally cooled air to help you beat the summer heat. Unlike other <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/air-conditioning/">air conditioning systems</a>, you can keep your doors and windows open when using your evaporative cooler. In fact, it's basically a requirement, because evaporative coolers continually cycle new air into your home from the outdoors.</p>
<p>Evaporative coolers work by taking in warm air from outdoors and running it over water-soaked cooling pads, which naturally lowers the temperature of the air. The cooled air is then circulated throughout the home, creating a refreshing breeze.</p>
<p>Evaporative coolers are more affordable, energy-efficient, and environmentally friendly than other air conditioners, such as <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/refrigerated-cooling/">refrigerated coolers</a>. The trade-off is reduced cooling performance compared to other systems, especially in humid conditions. However, during hot, dry summer days, which are most common in Melbourne, evaporative cooling will generally be a great option.</p>
