35:T650,<p>A split system air conditioner is a type of cooling that is split between an outdoor unit and an indoor unit, also known as the "head unit". These two units are connected by pipes and wiring, and they allow you to enjoy effective room cooling.</p><p>Because the noisiest part of the system is outside your home, you will enjoy quiet operation when using your system.</p>
<p>Traditional split system set-ups are designed to heat or cool one room at a time, or adjacent rooms if they're close enough together. These systems have one outdoor unit for every indoor unit, and each system operates independently.</p>
<p>Multi-head split system air conditioners allow you to <strong>run multiple indoor head units from a single outdoor unit</strong>. Just like with split systems, each indoor unit can be controlled independently, but you only have to worry about installing and maintaining a single outdoor unit.</p>
<p>Depending on the brand and set-up you choose, you can have <strong>5</strong><strong> or more indoor units connected to a single outdoor unit. </strong></p>
