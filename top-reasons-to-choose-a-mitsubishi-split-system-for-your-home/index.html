Whether you're experiencing the dead heat of summer or the coldest days of winter, a Mitsubishi Electric split system is a great way to stay cosy in your home or office.

Weather in Melbourne (and Australia in general) is hard to predict, so a good heating and cooling system will trump any seasonal changes. You’ll want to cool small and medium spaces with a versatile, affordable air conditioning solution when considering running costs, ease of use, and comfort. 

With state-of-the-art technology and reliable heating and cooling, this article will explain the top reasons why it's worth getting a Mitsubishi Electric split system.

If you've ever purchased a bulky floor air conditioner that requires tubing and extra steps, you'll know it can be a bit inconvenient. A split system, on the other hand, is usually attached to your wall, so there's no tricky installation booklet to go with it.

Let's dive into why it's worth the investment for any room in your home. 

You want to know that any split system you purchase will not cost you a fortune. Thankfully, Mitsubishi Electric split systems have high-efficiency fans and compressors that not only consume less power but also cool your home properly.

The R32 refrigerant feature has a higher level of energy efficiency than the R410A, for example. With one-third of the global warming potential, the risk of environmental harm is reduced.

Likewise, the Econo Cool Mode adjusts the direction of airflow based on the temperature you set, creating a comfortable space and adapting to the cooling needs of any room. Overall, when compared to gas and electric space heaters, Mitsubishi Electric offers reverse-cycle heating and cooling that is quiet, clean, and economical.

A great example of the Mitsubishi Electric energy-efficient system is the SUZ-M, MUZ-AP25-80 and MUZ-AS90 outdoor units, including a demand response enabling device (DRED). 

Your electricity provider can activate and control the system at three pre-programmed modes in response to signals during peak demand. This requires an additional adapter from your power provider, which is installed in accordance with AS/NZS 4755.3.1:2014.

01

Advanced Technology Features

What does a Mitsubishi Electric split system offer in terms of technology?

The full inverter compressor technology

This allows you to adjust the speed and power of your air conditioning unit based on the temperature you want for your spaces. 

For Mitsubishi Electric split systems, this outdoor unit DC scroll compressor (with the addition of a frame compliance mechanism) reduces the compressor's internal friction, increasing efficiency even in larger spaces.

I-Save Mode

A Mitsubishi Electric split system includes an I-Save Mode, which reduces power consumption. This simple setting function remembers your preferred (pre-set) temperature at the press of a button on the remote controller.

Quiet Mode 

This setting is added to the fan speed settings, giving you a super quiet operation as low as 18dB, which is perfect for your bedroom. You'll barely know it's turned on!

3D I-See sensor

This infra-red sensor includes eight vertically arranged sensor elements that analyse the room temperature in three dimensions.

The system can judge when people are present in the room, creating ‘indirect airflow’ to avoid airflow hitting people directly and ‘direct airflow’ to deliver airflow to where people are. When cooling your room, the middle of the room is detected to be warmer, and more airflow is directed towards it.

Night Mode 

This feature reduces the brightness of the operation indicator light, disables beeping noises, and limits the outdoor unit's noise level.

Free piping reuse technology

Mitsubishi Electric has a clean, free piping reuse technology, so you can reuse an existing refrigerant pipe and cut installation costs. The system is fitted with a 'wide strainer' that captures iron particles and prevents them from entering the outdoor unit.

Your Mitsubishi Electric will never use more power than it needs to, so you don't need to worry about a sudden influx in your energy bill.

02

Flexibility in Configurations

Ranging from wall-mounted to freestanding, ceiling cassette or ceiling suspended, Mitsubishi Electric split systems indoor unit offers true flexibility with a wide range of options to satisfy most application requirements.

Mitsubishi Electric systems have a vertical and horizontal vane design, so you can easily adjust the airflow direction and improve airflow. There is also a long airflow configuration, a high-power mode that combines with the long mode to push air further out.

Cold or warm air won't be contained in one small area but in every part of your space.

For example, the MSZ-AP Split System Air Conditioner is easy to operate, has clear instructions and will heat rooms even when all internal doors are open in your home.

Mitsubishi Electric split systems include advanced filtration systems such as the Plasma Quad Connect Air Purifying Filter. 

This optional filter connects to your air conditioner and improves air quality by inhibiting key pollutants such as Influenza, viruses, bacteria, PM2.5, allergens, dust, and mould. Experience a healthier environment, reassurance, and peace of mind at home.

These split systems also have Dual Barrier Coating, which prevents dust, greasy dirt, or airborne contaminants from collecting on the air conditioner's surface. Dirt is classified into two groups: hydrophilic dirt, such as fibre dust, and hydrophobic dirt, such as cigarette smoke.

This barrier coating has blended “fluorine particles” that stop this penetration, keeping your aircon clean.

Wi-Fi management allows you to manage your Mitsubishi Electric system for total home comfort. Connect your split system to your tablet, smartphone or online account and completely control your unit on the go from work, a friend's house, or anywhere in the world! Features include:

  • > Adjusting the set temperature
  • > Changing the mode
  • > Changing the fan speed
  • > Auto-off 
  • > Zone control

An optional Wi-Fi adjustment per unit requires an internet connection and the Wi-Fi Control App downloaded on your tablet or smartphone with the latest operating system. If you want to use Amazon Alexa, you'll need an Amazon Echo device.

With this remote temperature adjustment feature, scheduling, and mode changes, users can set the Mitsubishi Electric system to the desired temperature before arriving home or if they need to get somewhere quickly. Not only is it convenient, but you're saving way more on quarterly electric costs.

With Australian weather often taking you by surprise, knowing that a Mitsubishi Electric reverse cycle split system has a guaranteed operating range of -5 to 46 degrees Celsius is comforting.

04

Cost-effectiveness Over Time

A Mitsubishi Electric system is the best choice for lowering running costs and saving long-term energy on your electric bill. Many systems have an excellent star rating!

Individually control each split system unit and turn it off when not in use. Choose heating, cooling, fan mode for air circulation, or dry mode to extract moisture for the ideal temperature for every room; your air con always stays at the mode you set it to.

And since these systems can be bought between 2.0kw and 9.0kw, you can match the size of your space to the unit's capacity. No split system will overwork itself.

Along with its versatile features, the Mitsubishi Electric split system has a Blue Fin condenser, as well as an  anti-corrosion treatment applied to the outdoor unit. This slows down the corrosion process of the aluminium fins, which is caused by salt in the air, especially if you live in a coastal area. As a result, your system will just last a lot longer than a traditional space heater.
