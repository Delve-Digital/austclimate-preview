38:T43f,<p><strong><em>Reverse cycle air conditioning is a heater and cooler in one system!</em></strong> In cooling mode, a reverse cycle system functions as <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/refrigerated-cooling/">a refrigerated air conditioner</a>. In heating mode, it provides electrically-powered heating that's comfortable and efficient.</p>
<p>The secret ingredient in reverse cycle air conditioning is the <strong>heat pump</strong>. This device works in tandem with refrigerant to transfer heat from one place to another. In cooling mode, it removes heat and creates cool air. In heating mode, it reverses this cycle; heat is absorbed from outside and used to heat your home.</p>
<p>Other systems use gas or electricity to generate heat directly, but a <em>reverse cycle unit simply moves heat energy around.</em> This allows you to heat or cool your property using one device, and it also results in an incredibly efficient HVAC system.</p>
