As energy prices continue to rise across Victoria, many homeowners are looking for affordable ways to replace ageing heating and cooling systems with more energy-efficient alternatives. Fortunately, the Victorian Government's Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program can help reduce the upfront cost of eligible heating and cooling upgrades, making modern systems more accessible than ever.

However, one of the most common questions homeowners ask is: Who actually qualifies for the Victorian Heating and Cooling Upgrade Rebate? The answer depends on several factors, including your existing heating system, the type of equipment being installed, and whether the installation meets current VEU program requirements.

Understanding these criteria before planning your upgrade can help you make informed decisions and maximise the financial assistance available. 01

What Is the Victorian Heating and Cooling Upgrade Rebate?

The Victorian Heating and Cooling Upgrade Rebate forms part of the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program, an initiative designed to improve the energy efficiency of homes and businesses across the state.

Rather than offering a rebate for every new heating system, the program provides incentives for approved upgrades that replace older, less efficient equipment with eligible high-efficiency alternatives.

The aim is to help households reduce energy consumption, lower greenhouse gas emissions, and decrease long-term energy costs while making the transition to modern heating and cooling systems more affordable. Because the VEU program is regularly updated, rebate amounts and eligibility requirements may change over time.

That’s why it's always worth checking your eligibility before making any purchasing decisions.

Why More Victorians Are Looking at Heating Upgrades

Heating accounts for a significant portion of household energy use during Melbourne's colder months. Combined with rising energy prices, this has encouraged many homeowners to reassess the efficiency of their existing systems. Older gas heaters and ageing air conditioning systems tend to consume more energy than modern alternatives while requiring increasingly frequent servicing and repairs. Rather than continuing to invest in outdated equipment, many Victorian households are taking advantage of available government incentives to upgrade to more efficient systems that deliver improved comfort and lower ongoing running costs.

03

Who May Be Eligible for a VEU Rebate?

Eligibility for the Victorian Heating and Cooling Upgrade Rebate depends on the specific requirements of the Victorian Energy Upgrades program at the time your upgrade is completed. While every property is different, eligibility is generally influenced by factors such as:

  • > The type of heating or cooling system currently installed.
  • > Whether you're replacing eligible older equipment.
  • > The efficiency and approval status of the new system.
  • > Compliance with current VEU program guidelines.
  • > Installation by an appropriately accredited provider where required.

  Because eligibility rules can change, the best way to determine whether you qualify is to arrange an assessment before committing to a replacement system. An experienced installer can explain the current requirements and help identify the most suitable upgrade options for your home.

04

Which Heating and Cooling Systems Are Commonly Eligible?

Many homeowners are surprised to learn that not every heating system automatically qualifies for government incentives. In many cases, rebates are available when replacing eligible older heating systems with approved high-efficiency reverse cycle air conditioning systems. Reverse cycle technology is recognised for its exceptional efficiency because it transfers heat instead of generating it directly.

This allows homeowners to enjoy comfortable heating during winter while also providing efficient cooling throughout Melbourne's warmer months. Choosing an eligible system not only helps reduce installation costs through available rebates but can also lower long-term household energy consumption.

Rising Heating Costs Are Driving More Upgrade Decisions

One of the biggest reasons homeowners enquire about VEU rebates is the continued increase in household heating costs. Energy bills have become a major concern for many Victorian families, particularly during winter when heating systems operate for extended periods.

Older heating systems can become increasingly expensive to run as they age, making them less economical than modern high-efficiency alternatives. Many homeowners now consider more than just the purchase price of a new heating system. They also evaluate:

  • > Ongoing energy consumption.
  • > Expected running costs.
  • > Maintenance requirements.
  • > System lifespan.
  • > Overall efficiency.

  Upgrading to a modern energy-efficient system can help reduce these long-term costs while improving year-round comfort.

Preparing for Your Heating Upgrade

Before arranging a new heating system, it's helpful to gather information about your current equipment. Consider questions such as:

  • > How old is the existing system?
  • > Has it required frequent repairs?
  • > Are energy bills continuing to increase?
  • > Does the system heat the home effectively?
  • > Would heating and cooling from one system better suit your household?

  Answering these questions can help your installer recommend the most suitable replacement while also determining whether your property may qualify for available rebates.
