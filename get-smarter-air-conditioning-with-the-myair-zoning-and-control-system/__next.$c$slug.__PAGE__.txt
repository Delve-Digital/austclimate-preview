2d:T453,<p>Modern air conditioners come with a range of smart features, from <a href="/austclimate-preview/panasonic-and-nanoe-x-air-purification/">Nanoe X Air Purification in Panasonic split systems</a> to <a href="/austclimate-preview/how-does-reverse-cycle-air-conditioning-work/">electrically powered reverse cycle air conditioning</a>, which offers both heating and cooling.</p>
<p>MyAir is an air conditioning management system that's designed to enhance your new or existing air conditioner, adding smart zoning technology and other intelligent features.</p>
<p>MyAir is <strong><em>compatible with almost all </em></strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/refrigerated-cooling/"><strong><em>air conditioning brands in Melbourne</em></strong></a>, including major brands like <a href="/austclimate-preview/brands/daikin/">Daikin</a>, <a href="/austclimate-preview/brands/panasonic/">Panasonic</a>, <a href="/austclimate-preview/brands/lg/">LG</a>, and <a href="/austclimate-preview/brands/rinnai/">Rinnai</a>. It can be integrated with newly installed HVAC units or retrofitted for existing systems.</p>2e:T561,<p>Ducted air conditioning systems make it easy to heat or cool your entire home, but setting one temperature for every room can be limiting. Additionally, central air conditioning units typically don't allow you to target specific areas - it's either all on or all off.</p>
<p>With zoning technology, you can divide your property into zones that can be heated and cooled individually. Some zoning systems give you air flow control but not temperature control, while others force you to combine multiple unrelated rooms into a single 'zone'.</p>
<p>MyAir's advanced technology allows you to create up to 10 different zones, meaning you can create a zone for every room if you want to! Additionally, each zone can set its own temperature and airflow settings.</p>
<p>The Exact Air Regulator (EAR) used in MyAir Dampers lets you adjust individual room air flow levels in 5% increments. This allows you to create quieter, smoother, more even air in each zone.</p>
<p>MyAir's fan control settings will also adjust fan speed depending on the number of zones in use. This ensures the airflow is never too high or low, regardless of whether one zone or every zone is active.</p>
<p>The result of all this is greater comfort, fewer fights over the thermostat, increased energy efficiency, and lower energy bills with no money being wasted on climate control in empty rooms.</p>
