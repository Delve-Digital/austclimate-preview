28:T485,<p>A <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/split-systems/">split system</a> is one of the most common air conditioner types. This popular type of AC is designed for a single room and consists of an outdoor unit located on or near an external wall. This is connected to a wall-mounted indoor unit via a series of pipes and wires. </p>
<p>The outdoor unit primarily houses the condenser and compressor, as well as fans, valves, and refrigerant. The indoor unit is where you can find the evaporator coils, louvres, and air filters. The refrigerant travels between the outdoor unit and the indoor unit during operation.</p>
<p>If you want to maintain the perfect temperature in the bedroom, living room, or home office, split systems could be perfect for you. Some people use split ACs in addition to central units, such as <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/refrigerated-cooling/">refrigerated cooling</a> or <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/ducted-gas-heating/">gas heating</a>, to provide extra climate control.</p>
<p>Split systems are easy and affordable to install, especially compared to ducted air conditioners and ducted heating!</p>

29:T43f,<p>Reverse cycle air conditioning systems offer refrigerated cooling and electric-powered heating in one unit. These systems use devices known as heat pumps, which can reverse the standard refrigeration cycle. This allows the refrigerant in your system to be used for both heating and cooling purposes.</p><p>Simply switch between heating mode and cooling mode on your system.</p>
<p>Reverse cycle air conditioners are more energy efficient and affordable than conventional electrical and gas heaters. This is because conventional systems burn fuel to generate heat, while reverse cycle systems simply absorb heat from the air and add it to your home!</p><p>This results in much lower energy consumption and also makes reverse cycle air conditioners more environmentally friendly.</p>
