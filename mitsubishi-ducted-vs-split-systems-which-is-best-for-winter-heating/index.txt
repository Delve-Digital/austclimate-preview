<p>According to a <a href="https://hipages.com.au/article/how_much_does_ducted_air_conditioning_installation">Hipages cost guide</a>, ducted heating in 2025 can cost from $2000 for a small capacity system designed for apartment living and up to $10,000 for a system that can heat a larger home. Ducted installation specifically ranges from $5000 to $30,000. These prices are based on national averages. The type of system you choose will have an impact on its price, installation fees and running costs.</p>
<p>Split Systems in 2025 can cost from $600 to $4900, and the cost depends on the type of unit and the complexity of the installation, starting at $600 for a reverse cycle system up to $4900 for a multi-split system. Whether you choose to purchase from a company that offers supply and installation or buy a unit yourself, explore both options to see what works for you financially.<br/><br/><strong>What are some points to think about?</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>&gt; </strong>Installing a ducted system involves planning and modifying the home's infrastructure. Ducts must be installed in walls or ceilings, and the central unit has to be positioned in a suitable location, often in the roof space. This can be time-consuming.</li>
<li><strong>&gt; </strong>Split systems require a small hole in the wall to connect the indoor and outdoor units. They are a lot more suitable for retrofitting into existing homes without a big renovation!</li>

<p><strong>What does ducted heating energy efficiency look like?</strong> According to <a href="https://www.sustainability.vic.gov.au/">Sustainability Victoria</a>, a ducted reverse-cycle air conditioner (unzoned) in a medium house (160m2) has an efficiency rating of 1 star, an annual energy cost of $1848, and uses 34.7 GHG emissions (tonnes) over 10 years.</p>
