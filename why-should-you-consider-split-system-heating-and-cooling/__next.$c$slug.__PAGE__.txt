39:T680,<p>Heating and cooling systems are divided into two broad categories. There are central heating and cooling systems like <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/ducted-gas-heating/">ducted heating</a>, and there are space heaters and coolers, which are designed to heat or cool a single area.</p>
<p><em>Split system air conditioning is the gold standard for heating and cooling a single space</em>. The installation is simple, the operation is energy-efficient, and the results are unbeatable!</p>
<p>Split systems get their name because the system contains two units that are split between the indoors and the outdoors. The indoor unit, sometimes called the head unit, contains the fan and the coil. This unit is usually installed high on the wall for a sleek appearance.</p>
<p>The outdoor unit consists of the condenser and compressor. It is located near the indoor unit on the outside of your home; either neatly on the floor against a wall or discreetly mounted to the wall itself. </p>
<p>If you have a window air conditioner or a portable heater, a split system is the perfect upgrade for you. If you want an alternative to a ducted system, a split system air conditioner also has you covered. By installing multiple indoor units, you can enjoy heating and cooling throughout your home!</p>
<p><strong><em>Installing a </em></strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/split-systems/"><strong><em>split system in Melbourne</em></strong></a><strong><em> gives you year-round comfort. </em></strong>Even if you're experiencing Melbourne's infamous four seasons in one day, a split system can set a consistent indoor temperature that suits you. </p>
