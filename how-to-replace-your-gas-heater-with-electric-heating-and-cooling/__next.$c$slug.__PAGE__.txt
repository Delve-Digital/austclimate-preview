38:T498,<p>As you’ve probably heard, a major push is underway to address Victoria’s current dependence on natural gas. Effective Jan 1, 2024, legislation from the State Government has already banned gas connections for new builds, and more and more homeowners with existing gas connections are also following suit, looking to replace gas heaters with electric alternatives.</p>
<p>If you’re currently looking to make the switch, there are measures in place to help all homes go away from gas and make the switch to an electrified home. After all, electric appliances and reverse-cycle electric heating can easily be powered by renewable energy sources, and additional financial incentives are already available, such as <a href="https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/products/heating-and-cooling-discounts">Victorian Energy Upgrades for Households</a> through the <a href="https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/about-the-veu-program">VEU Program</a>.</p>

If you’re currently considering electric instead of gas heating for your property, these are some of the major advantages and benefits:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Improved indoor air quality</strong>. Electric heating doesn't involve combustion like gas furnaces, and this stops the release of by-products such as carbon monoxide and other pollutants, leading to a healthier living space.</li>
<li><strong>Flexible installation</strong>. Electric furnaces don't need a gas line, so this simplifies the installation process. It is less of a hassle if you want to adapt or upgrade your system, as there are no major infrastructure changes!</li>
<li><strong>Adaptable to renewable energy</strong>. As high-efficiency gas furnaces are a thing of the past, electric heating is a sustainable and eco-friendly option. Homeowners can power electric systems with clean fuel sources, such as solar power.</li>
<li><strong>Save money</strong>. Depending on your state or territory, reduced energy costs, lower maintenance requirements, and government incentives can save you a chunk of cash in the long term.</li>
<li><strong>Less maintenance</strong>. Electric furnaces have fewer components prone to wear and tear, so your family can enjoy the benefits of electric heaters without servicing them regularly.</li>
<li><strong>Experience the latest technology</strong>. Integrating into your spaces with Smart Home Technology, your family can use programmable thermostats, remote control, or compatibility with home automation systems. Heat the home more easily.</li>
</ul>
