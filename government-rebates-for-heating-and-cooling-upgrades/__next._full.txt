<p>So, who exactly is eligible for this solar power rebate? Firstly, you must be a homeowner, landlord, or tenant with the landlord's permission. This ensures that the property where the solar panel system is installed is legally yours or you have obtained necessary permissions.</p>
<p>The next criterion is that the solar panel system should be installed by an approved retailer. This is one of the most important eligibility requirements. This criterion aims to ensure that the solar panel systems installed are of top quality, safe, and efficient.</p><p>It helps to prevent substandard installations and protects consumers from rogue installers offering cheap but ineffective solar panel systems.</p>
<p>Finally, a few more requirements: </p>
<p></p>
<ul>
<ul><li>Combined household taxable income is less than $210,000 per year</li>
<li>the property is valued at under $3 million, or a home under construction that will be valued at under $3 million</li>
<li>the property address has not previously received a solar panel rebate or a solar battery rebate under the Solar Homes Program</li>
<li>the property has not had a solar panel system installed after 1 November 2009</li>
</ul>
<p>More information can be found on the Solar Victoria website <a href="https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/solar-panel-rebate">on the Solar Victoria website</a>.</p>
