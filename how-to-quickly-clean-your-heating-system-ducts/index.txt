27:T5d6,<p>Dust and dirt lurk in every property, even if you think you have a particularly clean home. When you use your gas ducted heating or reverse cycle heating and cooling, tiny particles of dust will get sucked into the system and accumulate in your ducts.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, dusty and dirty ducts are just the beginning of your problem. Dust in your ducted heating system can lead to a build-up of mould and microbiological growth, and it can attract pests such as rodents and insects. Dust can also affect other parts of your heating system such as the fans and coils.</p><p>This will result in decreased efficiency, reduced performance, higher running costs, and a shorter overall life for your heater. You will not only face higher costs in the short-term but increased expenses when you need to prematurely service, repair or replace your system.</p>
<p>If anyone living or working in your property has asthma, allergies, or dust sensitivities, duct cleaning is even more important, as cleaning out your ducts helps to maintain a high level of indoor air quality.</p><p>Additionally, if you have completed home renovations that have generated excess levels of dirt and dust, it's also a good idea to clean out your ducted system.</p>
<p>Overall, dirty ducted heating systems can be bad for your health, your property, and your HVAC system. Whether you've noticed specific issues or you’ve just never cleaned out your ducts before, duct cleaning is an important step to take.</p>

28:T611,<p>DIY duct cleaning is a great way to go if you're just taking preventative measures and you haven't noticed any glaring problems with your ducted heating system.</p>
<p>Here are<i> 6 simple tips you can follow to clean your ducted heating system yourself:</i></p>
<p>1. Quality filters are essential for your ducted heating. Your system should be fitted with an air filter that is changed twice a year. 2. To clean your ducted heating, start by removing the vents, grilles, or grates where the hot air comes out.</p><p>You can wash these in warm soapy water and let them dry through a combination of towel drying and air drying.</p><p>3. Once the vents are removed, you can reach into your ducts and remove any large objects by hand. If you have young children or pets and your ducts are located on the floor, don't be surprised to find items such as pencils, small toys, or even a hidden doggy bone.</p><p>4. The next thing to do is clean out any smaller dust and debris that you can reach using a vacuum cleaner. You might also want to use a torch to make sure that you suck up as much as possible. 5.</p><p>There might also be mess in your ducts that can't be taken up by a vacuum, such as sticky residue caused by a spill.</p><p>To remove these, clean the area thoroughly with a wet cloth. You should then dry the area as much as you can and leave it to air out before reattaching the air vent and using the system. 6.</p><p>If you have additional filters in your grates, wash and dry these out regularly and replace them as necessary.</p>

29:T712,<p>If you feel that your ducted heating issue is a little more complicated, maybe it’s time to call in the professionals for a thorough air duct cleaning service.</p>
<p>Here are a few situations where calling the professionals for duct cleaning is definitely the right call:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mould:</strong> If you discover mould when you're doing your DIY duct cleaning, it's best to call the professionals to ensure it is expertly treated. Mould that is not professionally removed can come back in no time at all.</li>
<li><strong>Ducted Heating Allergies: </strong>If you or someone else has allergy symptoms every time the ducted heating is switched on, professional duct cleaning may be required. This is especially the case if you have tried cleaning the system yourself and allergic reactions persist. In all cases related to health, it's also important to seek medical advice.</li>
<li><strong>Excessive Dust: </strong>If you've noticed a large build-up of dust or dust is spilling out of your vents and onto the floor, this is a sign that you should have your ducts looked at by a professional duct cleaner.</li>
<li><strong>Pest Infestation:</strong> If you can see or hear rodents or insects in your ducted heating, professionals will be needed to clean your ductwork and remove the infestation.</li>
<li><strong>Moving home: </strong>Professional duct cleaning is a sure-fire way to make sure the ducted heating in your new home is ready to perform at its best. This is particularly important if you have purchased an older property.</li>
