Do you want to install a Heat Pump Hot Water system on your residential property? This system leverages ambient air to heat water, resulting in energy savings.

Victoria's climate makes Heat Pump Hot Water systems an ideal choice. Even in cooler temperatures, these systems draw heat from the air, giving you consistent hot water despite external conditions. This makes them an environmentally friendly and cost-effective choice.

Rinnai

Rinnai heat pumps offer energy-efficient, low running cost hot water solutions. Designed for all climates, they feature quiet operation, anti-frost protection, PV compatibility, and environmentally sustainable performance.

Haier

Haier heat pumps offer an energy-efficient way to heat water, reducing electricity use by up to 79%. With smart technology, low-impact refrigerants, and solar PV compatibility, they prioritise sustainability and control.

Midea

Midea heat pumps use advanced technology to harvest renewable energy from the air, reducing energy use by up to 70%. With impressive efficiency, they provide reliable hot water while saving costs.

Energy Efficiency and Government Incentives

One of the major advantages of Heat Pump Hot Water systems is their energy efficiency. Making use of ambient air for heating, these systems require less electrical energy than standard electric hot water systems, leading to lower energy usage and bills.
