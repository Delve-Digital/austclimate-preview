<ul>
<li>&gt; Not cleaning your heater doesn’t put a stop to debris/dust building up inside of it, which results in component failure or overheating</li>
<li>&gt; Moving parts that aren’t lubricated increase friction and unnecessary wear and tear</li>
<li>&gt; A burner that isn’t clean or fully functional puts more strain on the heating system</li>
<li>&gt; The thermostat or pressure valve won’t work properly</li>
</ul>
<p></p>

38:T5d0,<p>From August 10, 2022, the installation of any new or replacement gas ducted heaters that do not comply with the updated Australian Standard AS5263.1.3 <strong>is not permitted in Victoria</strong>. For example, it is illegal to sell, supply or install new or second-hand open-flued gas space heaters that do not meet new and improved safety specifications.</p>
<p>Some ducted heaters are open-flued and can be affected by negative pressure if there is insufficient ventilation.</p><p>This applies to ducted heaters when the appliance is installed indoors, and if this pressure affects the ducted heater, the air ducts around the house that push out hot air are not affected by the operation of exhaust fans.</p>
<p>If your gas heater does not comply with current Australian safety standards, your home will not be safe. Older heaters with open-flued technology, for example, may not meet the latest safety requirements in Victoria for both public residences and public housing to protect all properties.</p>
<p>Regular servicing is non-negotiable. According to <a href="https://www.energysafe.vic.gov.au/">Energy Safe Victoria</a>, your gas heater should be serviced at least every two years by a qualified gasfitter, and a carbon dioxide test should be conducted at the heating vent outlets in all rooms. Some heaters need fresh air flow to operate safely. This way, you’ll lower your chances of dangerous incidents and keep your spaces on board with legal requirements.</p>
