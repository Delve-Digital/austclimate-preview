29:T61c,<p><strong>Gas heating is powered by either LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) or natural gas</strong>. In Melbourne, natural gas is by far the more common option.</p>
<p>Gas heating systems generate heat energy by drawing air into a furnace. The furnace heats this air in a process called gas combustion, and the warm air is then circulated throughout your house for cosy comfort during those cold days and nights.</p>
<p>The most common form of gas heating is <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/ducted-gas-heating/"><strong>gas ducted heating</strong></a>. These systems use a series of ducts to distribute warm air throughout your property for whole-home heating. They remain incredibly popular in cold climates such as Melbourne.</p>
<p>Other options for gas heating include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Gas space heaters:</strong> These gas heaters are ideal for heating a single room. Gas space heaters like the <strong>Rinnai Energysaver</strong> send warm air out into the room just like a gas ducted system. <strong>Flued and unflued gas heaters are available</strong>. </li>
<li><strong>Gas log fires: </strong>These heaters also use gas combustion to warm up your home. Instead of spreading heat via hot air, a gas log fire relies on the radiant heat from flames for its heat output. Gas log fires are the heating of choice for families who want an aesthetically appealing centrepiece that also heats the room. These systems can be installed by retrofitting an existing fireplace, creating a false fireplace, or installing a weatherproof box</li>
</ul>

2a:T780,<p>When it comes to <strong>electric heating</strong>, there are two main types to consider.</p>
<p>If you choose an electric furnace, your heating will work in a similar way to gas heating. The only difference is that the <strong>heat is produced using an electric heating element via electrical resistance</strong> rather than gas combustion.</p>
<p>This kind of heating is common in <strong>portable forced-air electric heaters</strong> and <strong>electric convection heaters</strong>. These systems have a bad reputation because of their high heating costs.</p>
<p>The other type of electric unit is a <strong>heat pump system.</strong> These systems are most common in <strong>reverse cycle heating and cooling</strong>.</p>
<p>A heat pump system warms the home by absorbing heat from the outside air and distributing it around a room or throughout your property. In contrast to other electric heating systems, <strong>heat pumps are incredibly energy-efficient and affordable</strong>. They can operate at more than 100% efficiency and boast a small carbon footprint.</p>
<p>Some of the most popular options for heat pump systems in Melbourne include:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/split-systems/"><strong>Split systems</strong></a><strong>:</strong> These systems can heat or cool individual rooms in the home and are comprised of an indoor unit and an outdoor unit. For heating and cooling in multiple rooms, you can also choose <strong>multi-split systems</strong>, which run several indoor units powered by a single outdoor unit</li>
<li><a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/reverse-cycle-heating/"><strong>Ducted Reverse Cycle Air Conditioning</strong></a><strong>:</strong> These heat pump systems can warm or cool your entire property by distributing air through a series of ducts.
