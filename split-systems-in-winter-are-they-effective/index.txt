26:T463,<p><em>Not all split system air conditioners are designed with a heating and cooling mode.</em> If you want a split system that works in winter, <strong>look for a reverse cycle split system (also known as a heat pump system).</strong></p>
<p><em>So, how do reverse cycle air conditioners work?</em></p>
<p>In heating mode, they absorb heat energy from the air outside your property using a heat exchanger. This heat is then transferred inside your home. The condenser in your outdoor unit also heats the refrigerant, which further warms the air that has been absorbed from outside.</p>
<p>By the time it reaches your living room or bedroom, the air has been perfectly heated and filtered, allowing you to enjoy clean, warm, and comfortable indoor temperatures.</p>
<p>The process to cool your home using a split system is essentially the reverse of this. That's why heat pump units are referred to as reverse cycle air conditioners!</p>
<p>As well as split reverse cycle ACs, you can also get <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/reverse-cycle-heating/">reverse cycle heating and cooling in a ducted unit.</a></p>
