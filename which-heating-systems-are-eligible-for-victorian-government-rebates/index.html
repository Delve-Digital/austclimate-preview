As energy prices continue to rise, Victorian homeowners are looking for ways to improve the efficiency of their homes while reducing the cost of upgrading ageing heating systems. One of the most effective ways to make a new heating system more affordable is through the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program.

The VEU program provides financial incentives that can significantly reduce the upfront cost of installing eligible energy-efficient heating systems. For homeowners replacing older gas heating, these rebates can make upgrading to modern technology far more accessible.

However, not every heating system qualifies for a rebate. Understanding which systems are eligible (and the conditions that apply) can help you make a smarter investment for your home.

At Australian Climate Solutions, we provide professional heating services, sales & installation, and expert guidance on available VEU rebates, helping homeowners choose energy-efficient heating solutions that deliver lasting comfort and value.

01

What Is the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) Program?

The Victorian Energy Upgrades program is a Victorian Government initiative designed to reduce household energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions by encouraging the installation of more energy-efficient appliances and systems.

Rather than offering a one-size-fits-all rebate, the program provides incentives for approved upgrades that replace older, less efficient equipment with modern alternatives.

For homeowners, this can mean significantly lower installation costs when replacing eligible heating systems with approved energy-efficient products.

As rebate amounts and eligibility requirements can change over time, it's always important to confirm the latest information before planning your upgrade.

02

Why More Victorians Are Taking Advantage of VEU Rebates

There are several reasons why demand for VEU-supported heating upgrades has increased in recent years.

One of the biggest factors is the continued rise in household energy costs. Many older heating systems consume considerably more energy than modern alternatives, making them increasingly expensive to operate.

Homeowners are also recognising the benefits of upgrading ageing equipment before it becomes unreliable or requires costly repairs.

By combining government rebates with lower ongoing running costs, many households find that replacing an older heating system makes strong financial sense over the long term.

03

Which Heating Systems Are Eligible?

The exact systems eligible under the Victorian Energy Upgrades program depend on current government guidelines and approved product lists.

In many cases, eligible homeowners replacing older gas heating systems can access rebates when installing approved reverse-cycle heating systems.

Reverse cycle technology is recognised for its exceptional energy efficiency because it transfers heat rather than generating it directly. This allows homeowners to enjoy reliable winter heating while using significantly less energy than many older systems.

Eligibility typically depends on several factors, including:

  • > The existing heating system being replaced
  • > The type of replacement system
  • > Whether approved equipment is installed
  • > Installation by an authorised provider where required
  • > Current VEU program requirements


Because these conditions may change over time, professional advice can help ensure your upgrade meets all applicable criteria.

04

Why Reverse Cycle Heating Is Often the Preferred Upgrade

Reverse cycle heating has become one of the most popular choices for Victorian homeowners, and for good reason.

Unlike traditional heating systems that only provide warmth during winter, reverse cycle systems offer year-round climate control by providing both heating and cooling from a single installation.

Additional benefits include:

  • > Outstanding energy efficiency
  • > Lower running costs
  • > Fast, responsive heating
  • > Quiet operation
  • > Precise temperature control
  • > Reduced maintenance compared to many ageing systems


These advantages make reverse cycle heating an ideal solution for Melbourne's variable climate, where homeowners often require both heating and cooling throughout the year.

05

Rising Heating Costs Are Driving More Homeowners to Upgrade

Heating has become one of the largest contributors to winter energy bills for many Victorian households.

With electricity and gas prices remaining a major consideration, homeowners are increasingly looking beyond the upfront cost of a new heating system and focusing on long-term operating expenses.

Older gas heating systems can become particularly expensive to run as they age, especially if they require regular servicing or repairs.

Modern reverse cycle systems offer an energy-efficient alternative that helps reduce ongoing household energy consumption while delivering excellent comfort throughout winter.

When combined with available government rebates, upgrading to a more efficient system can offer both immediate savings on installation and reduced running costs for years to come.

06

Can Existing Gas Heating Be Replaced?

Yes, in many cases, replacing an ageing gas heating system with an approved reverse cycle system is exactly the type of upgrade encouraged under the VEU program.

Many Victorian homeowners are choosing to move away from older gas heaters for several reasons:

  • > Improved energy efficiency
  • > Lower household energy costs
  • > Reduced maintenance requirements
  • > Heating and cooling from one system
  • > Access to available government rebates


Replacing an ageing gas heater before it fails also allows homeowners to plan their upgrade rather than facing an emergency replacement during winter.
