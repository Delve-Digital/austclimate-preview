20:Tc17,<p>Daikin has been a prominent name in the Australian heating and cooling industry for years, and with good reason. From humble beginnings, delivering water-cooled air conditioners in Sydney in 1969, the company has grown immensely and now is a household name around the country. </p>
<p>Are you interested to learn what makes Daikin a huge hit among homeowners? Keep scrolling.</p>
<p><b>Outstanding Products</b></p>
<p>Creating a good product is one thing, but making one that lasts is another. One of the best things about Daikin is their commitment to quality. Their air conditioning models, such as the <a href="/austclimate-preview/media/9f77dacfc7a8-Daikin_Split-Brochure-AU.pdf">Daikin Split Systems</a>, always offer more than you’d expect.</p>
<p>Additional functions (like smartphone control) and beneficial features (like National Asthma Council Sensitive Choice® certification) are what makes these units stand out. Daikin is a market leader when it comes to efficient, clean split system air conditioning systems, and we are happy to give you more detail if you’d like to give us a call.</p>
<p><b>Sustainable Technology</b></p>
<p>Air conditioning companies always get a bad rep for ecological reasons. But with Daikin, we might just be ushering in a new era of innovation.</p>
<p>Their next-gen air conditioners are loaded with R-32, a refrigerant known to reduce power consumption and produce an even lower environmental impact. It was an extremely difficult refrigerant to handle until Daikin broke through as the first company to ever use R-32 on air conditioners. At Australian Climate Systems, we are happy to outline the benefits of R-32 for you.<b> </b></p>
<p><b>Global Philosophy</b></p>
<p>Becoming an industry giant doesn’t happen overnight. Daikin has put in a lot of work to build up to the stage where the company is a global leader with cutting edge technology.</p>
<p>Since its founding, the Japan-based brand has continuously evolved to keep up with changing demands. Its Group Philosophy is even centred around three key corporate principles: <b><i>absolute credibility</i></b>, <b><i>enterprising management</i></b> and <b><i>harmonious personal relations</i></b>.</p>
<p>Today, Daikin is an industry leader in terms of cooling and heating. Their proven experience continues to wow people all over the world as a well-trusted brand that delivers beyond expectations.</p>
