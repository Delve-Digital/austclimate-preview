24:T44c,<p>There's nothing quite like shopping for a new home. It can be equally exciting, stressful, and emotional, which can become incredibly overwhelming! It's easy to get swept up in the viewings or bogged down in the numbers, but it's important to focus on the details.</p>
<p><a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/"><strong>Heating</strong></a><strong> </strong>and<strong> </strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/"><strong>cooling</strong></a> are responsible for keeping us comfortable all year round. As well as this, <em>climate control accounts for up to 50% of your energy consumption</em>, meaning it will contribute significantly to your household energy bills.</p>
<p>Before you commit to buying a home, you need to understand what type of heating and cooling is available, what condition it is in, and what this means for you. </p>
<p>Homeownership is the most significant financial commitment most of us will make in a lifetime. To make sure your investment is a wise one, <strong><em>remember these heating and cooling considerations when shopping for a new home.</em></strong></p>25:Ta7a,<p>When it comes to HVAC systems, you need to know whether you're getting heating, cooling, or both. It's not always obvious at first glance, so be sure to ask the real estate agent for clarification. They should know - and if they don't - they should be able to find out.</p>
<p>Some heating systems to look for include <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/ducted-gas-heating/"><strong>gas ducted heating</strong></a><strong>, </strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/hydronic-heating/"><strong>hydronic heating</strong></a><strong>, </strong>and<strong> </strong>gas log fires<strong>.</strong></p>
<p>Cooling systems include <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/evaporative-cooling-systems/"><strong>evaporative cooling</strong></a><strong>, </strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/refrigerated-cooling/"><strong>refrigerated air conditioning</strong></a><strong>, </strong>and<strong> </strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/split-systems/"><strong>split systems</strong></a><strong>.</strong></p>
<p>Some systems, like <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/reverse-cycle-heating/"><strong>ducted reverse cycle air conditioners</strong></a><strong>,</strong> offer both heating and cooling. Many split system air conditioners are reverse cycle, but others are cooling only, and this isn't always obvious at first glance. To find out, you can look for stickers or labels. You can also try to access a heating mode on the remote, or you can ask the agent for more information.</p>
<p>Another option for combined heating and cooling is <strong>gas ducted heating with </strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/add-on-air-condition/"><strong>add-on refrigerated air conditioning</strong></a>. These systems use the same ductwork as your gas heater, but they will have a separate outdoor unit. If a gas ducted heater doesn't have add-on cooling, you can note down the model and find out if it is compatible.</p>
<p>Of course, the home you're looking at might also have separately installed heating and cooling systems that operate completely independently. Some older homes may not even have a proper cooling system, relying solely on a ceiling fan or portable coolers. </p>
<p>Another thing to consider is gas vs electric.
