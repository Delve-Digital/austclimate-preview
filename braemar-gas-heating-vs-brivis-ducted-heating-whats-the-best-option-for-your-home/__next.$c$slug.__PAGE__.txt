32:T613,<p>Never used gas heating before in your home? Let's look at what it is and how it works! </p>
<p>Powered by natural gas, a gas heater warms up your space through a series of ducts–sometimes called silver pipes.</p>
<p>Outdoor units pass through cold air into a heat exchanger, entering your room through vents or outlets attached to these ducts. This can be extremely energy-efficient as it heats the home with a single unit. However, there’s still an option for multi-head split systems</p>
<p>What is the best part of ducted heating? Heat output can be managed with a nifty hand controller and cleverly positioned outside or in your roofing space and connected to outlets throughout your property. When spaces reach a happy temperature, the heater switches off once all the hot air inside the unit has been used.</p>
<p><strong>What are some of the advantages of gas heating?</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>&gt;</strong> Compared to an unfluted gas heater, gas ducted systems warm the home quickly and don't let air escape. You won’t feel any cold spots if you move to another room.</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>&gt;</strong> Ducted gas heating has zoning options, so you can turn off heating in rooms you don't use.</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>&gt;</strong> A refrigerated cooling system (called add-on cooling) can be attached to heaters with a higher star rating.</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>&gt;</strong> There are many models to choose from, and efficiency ratings supporting your needs for heating.</li>
</ul>
