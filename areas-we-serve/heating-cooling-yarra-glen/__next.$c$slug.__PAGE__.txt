29:T405,<p>Weak airflow, unusual noises, water leaks, repeated cycling or rooms failing to reach the selected temperature should be investigated rather than ignored. Common causes include blocked filters, dirty coils, failing fans, electrical faults, refrigerant issues and damaged ductwork.</p>
<p>An inspection should identify the source of the problem before deciding whether a component needs repairing or the entire system should be replaced. Replacement can make sense when an older system has frequent breakdowns, declining efficiency or difficult-to-source parts.
