Best Air Conditioning for Kilsyth Family Homes

Heating and cooling a family home in Kilsyth comes with its own set of considerations. You may want to customise the temperature in different rooms of the house to ensure everyone’s comfort, or prioritise energy-efficiency.

Ducted Systems: Ducted air conditioning is ideal for larger homes. Benefits of ducted systems include:

  • Zoning Capability: Many ducted systems allow you to choose which rooms are heated or cooled for maximum energy efficiency.
  • Advanced Features: Inverter technology adjusts its output to suit the current needs of the room, while features like WiFi control let you conveniently adjust your settings.
  • Sleek Design: With ductwork hidden away, ducted systems are a sleek, unobtrusive heating and cooling solution.

At Australian Climate Systems, we can retrofit ducted systems into older homes as well as installing it in new builds. Contact us today to enquire about installing ducted air conditioning in Kilsyth!

Split Systems

If you live in a smaller home, don’t need to heat or cool your whole home at once, or are looking for a lower-cost heating and cooling solution, a split system might be the match for you. Split systems are:

  • Energy Efficient: Cooling just one or two rooms at once uses less energy, helping you save on your power bill.
  • Space-Saving: Multi-head split systems let you cool multiple rooms, with the indoor units connected to just one outdoor unit.
  • Easy to Install: Installing a split system is very straightforward and requires minimal disruption to your schedule.

We supply and install quality split system air conditioners from brands like Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, LG, Panasonic, and more.

The Benefits of Installing Refrigerated Cooling in Kilsyth

Summer in Kilsyth brings with it occasional humidity and heatwaves. Refrigerated cooling in Kilsyth is ideal, allowing you to adjust your indoor climate to your heart’s content even when temperatures soar.

Additionally, most refrigerated cooling systems feature reverse cycle technology, so you can heat and cool your home with the same system. An air conditioner that offers heating as well as cooling will be particularly welcome on those cool, foggy mornings in winter.

Live in an older home with gas ducted heating and want to upgrade to refrigerated cooling? We can install add-on cooling, or upgrade you to an energy-efficient reverse cycle system. Book a consultation to learn more.
