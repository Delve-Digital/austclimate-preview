31:T4fd,<p>There is a wide range of heating and cooling products available, but in this article, we're talking specifically about <strong>ducted heater servicing</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Ducted heating</strong> is a central heating solution, meaning you can heat your whole home at once. Ducted heaters utilise a series of concealed ducts, vents and grilles in your home, and a furnace that lives outdoors.</p>
<p>Most of the time, the term ducted heating refers to gas ducted heating. A <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/ducted-gas-heating/"><strong>ducted gas heating system</strong></a> uses gas combustion to heat the air. </p>
<p>Ducted heating may also refer to <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/reverse-cycle-heating/"><strong>reverse cycle air conditioning</strong></a> (or reverse cycle heating and cooling). Ducted reverse cycle systems utilise heat pump technology to heat your home on cold days and keep it cool on warm days.</p>
<p>Ducted heaters stand apart from solutions like <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/split-systems/">split system heating</a> or gas log fires, which are designed to provide heating only for one room.</p>
<p>Now you know a bit more about ducted heating and how it works, let's determine the best way to take care of it.</p>
<p></p>32:T498,<p>If you don't service your ducted heating unit regularly, it will be dust!</p>
<p>We don't just mean that your heater will stop working either. What we mean is that your ducted heater will literally become full of dust, and this can lead to a range of problems.</p>
<p>You see, ducted heating systems work by recirculating the same air throughout your home. The air is sucked in, heated up, and blown back out of the vents. Your ducted heating system will have filters, but these will become clogged over time. </p>
<p><em>A build-up of dust can affect many internal components of your air conditioner</em>, and while it may not cause it to completely break down, it will force your heater to work harder to produce the same (or even worse) heating results. This means more wear and tear, higher running costs, a less energy-efficient system, a greater likelihood of breakdown, and a shorter overall life for your heater.</p>
