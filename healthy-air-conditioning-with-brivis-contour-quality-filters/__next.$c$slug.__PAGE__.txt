3a:T527,<p>According to a <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18492050/">study</a> conducted in 2008, contaminated AC units can worsen the condition of people with asthma and allergies. This <i>“sick building syndrome”</i> is a phenomenon comprising various symptoms we are all too aware of; nasal congestion, breathing problems, headaches, fatigue, and even irritated skin.</p>
<p>The problem here is less about the air conditioning itself and more about the contaminants present in the space. The closed-off nature of most air conditioning systems allows contaminants to continually circulate.</p>
<p>As well as this, poorly maintained cooling systems create the perfect breeding ground for microorganisms. This is because the process of cooling hot air creates an ample amount of moisture and condensation.</p>
<p>Individuals who are suffering from an acute or chronic respiratory disease will also be at risk of worsened cold, flu, and allergy-like symptoms when exposed to poorly maintained AC systems or air conditioners that are operating in an unhealthy environment.</p>
<p>Even for completely healthy individuals, very cold air or sudden temperature changes can affect the respiratory system and trigger symptoms.</p>
