2b:T54f,<p>Floor-standing air conditioners are essentially a variation of the classic wall-mounted split system. While most split system installations involve mounting the indoor unit high on the wall - usually towards the ceiling - these floor-standing units are mounted low on the wall or on the floor itself.</p><p>A corresponding outdoor unit is mounted low on an exterior wall or the ground.</p>
<p>Floor-standing air conditioning may also be referred to as floor-mounted air conditioning. There can be a difference between systems that stand on the floor and systems that are mounted on or near the floor, but for the most part, we will refer to them interchangeably in this article. </p>
<p>Floor-standing and wall-mounted units work in essentially the same way. Refrigerant travels between the outdoor unit and the indoor unit to produce cold air in cooling mode or hot air in heating mode.</p><p>Floor-standing air conditioners are generally smaller than their wall-mounted counterparts, but they come with all the same standard features, such as compressors, heat exchangers, condenser coils, evaporators, inverters, filters, and fans. </p>
<p>You are more likely to find a floor-standing unit in a hotel, motel, or community hall than a family home, but there are circumstances where a floor unit would be suitable for a residential dwelling.</p>
