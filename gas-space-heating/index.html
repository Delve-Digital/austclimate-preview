Heating for individual rooms

Australian Climate Systems has a range of Gas Space Heaters available to suit any room in a variety of configurations.

From the standard Braemar WF30 to the amazing Braemar Eco-Superstar 4-8 star space heater, and now the new 4 star Pyrox power flue wall furnace we are sure to have something to suit your needs.

Wall furnace

Braemar WF30 wall furnace

The Braemar WF30 wall furnace is an ideal replacement for existing wall furnaces and is able to heat up to 90m2.2.

Space heater

Braemar Eco-Superstar

Braemar eco-superstar space heaters are available in two sizes to meet your heating needs. The SH18 heats up to 62m2 and the SH25 unit heats up to 85m2.

An ultra-efficient Braemar SH25 space heater has an impressive 4.8 star rating and is a perfect solution for those wanting super-efficient and economical heating.

Wall furnace

Braemar WF25 wall furnace

The ultra-efficient Braemar WF25 wall furnace has an impressive 4.8 star rating, heats up to 85m2 and is the perfect solution for those wanting super-efficient and economical heating.
