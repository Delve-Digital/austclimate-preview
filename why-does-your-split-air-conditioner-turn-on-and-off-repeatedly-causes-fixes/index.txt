25:T410,<p>Short cycling means it takes longer for your room to reach its set temperature, you’ll use more energy to reach this temperature, and your AC will work twice as much when cooling or heating. </p>
<p>Much like driving a car, you wouldn’t drive for 10 minutes, stop for 10 minutes and then start driving again. You’d use more fuel, create more wear and tear on your car, and never get to your destination! When your AC short cycles, the impact includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>&gt; Energy usage costs rise</strong> since your air conditioner is working harder. It's using more energy than a functioning AC!</li>
<li><strong>&gt; There will be higher levels of humidity in your home</strong>. An AC is supposed to remove heat and humidity, so when a unit has short cycles, humidity in the air is trapped within the walls of your home. </li>
<li><strong>&gt; Wear on your HVAC</strong>. Compressors and aircon have an estimated life span of up to 20 years (when maintained) but short cycling wears it down quicker. </li>
</ul>
