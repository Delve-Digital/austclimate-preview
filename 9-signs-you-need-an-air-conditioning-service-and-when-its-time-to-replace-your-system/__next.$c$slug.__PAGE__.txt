33:T50b,<p>Regular maintenance is one of the most important steps you can take in caring for your split system or ducted air conditioner. While you might baulk at the costs at first, a poorly maintained air conditioner will only end up costing you more in the long term.</p>
<p>Coolers that are faulty or in poor condition will have to work harder to produce the same amount of cold air. They are also more prone to sudden breakdowns, which can lead to expensive air conditioner repair and maintenance costs.</p><p>In extreme cases, a faulty air conditioner can lead to water damage in your roof, house fires, or other dangerous and costly situations.</p>
<p>Even if your air conditioner appears to be functioning properly, it could be causing your electricity bills to skyrocket in the summer months. In some cases, your air conditioner will be costing you more money but producing less effective results, which is the ultimate lose-lose scenario.</p>
<p>If you're lucky, your scheduled air conditioning service will come around and you won't notice any adverse effects. However, if you notice any of the signs listed below, you should arrange <a href="/austclimate-preview/services/services-and-maintenance/"><strong>air conditioner servicing</strong></a><strong> </strong>as soon as possible.</p>34:T446,<p><strong>How often should you service your air conditioning unit? </strong></p>
<p>The answer will vary depending on who you ask, and many air conditioning professionals recommend yearly services. You certainly can't go wrong with a yearly service, but here at <a href="/austclimate-preview/">Australian Climate Systems</a>, <strong>we recommend servicing your cooler every 2-3 years.</strong></p>
<p>Of course, servicing time can vary depending on a range of factors, including manufacturer recommendations and warranties, your level of usage, and commercial operation versus residential operation.</p>
<p>If it has been 2-3 years or longer since your last service, we highly recommend calling us today and booking a service. Whether you need a <strong>split system service, evaporative cooling service, or air conditioning repair</strong>, we can assist you.</p>
