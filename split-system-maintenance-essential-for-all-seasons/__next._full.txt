23:T5e4,<p>A split system air conditioner consists of two main sections: the indoor unit and the outdoor unit. The indoor unit, which is the part you see inside your home, is responsible for circulating cool air.</p><p>It houses the evaporator coil, which absorbs the heat from the air, and a fan, which then blows this cooled air into your space.</p><p>The outdoor unit contains the compressor and condenser coil, along with another fan, all of which work together to remove the heat from the refrigerant and blow it out.</p>
<p>But why split the units? Traditional air conditioners can produce a lot of noise due to the compressor's location. In a split system, the compressor is located outside, significantly reducing noise levels inside your home or business. This makes split-system air conditioners a popular choice for bedrooms, offices, and even libraries. </p>
<p>Additionally, with a multi-head split system, you can get multiple indoor units for different rooms, all powered by the same outdoor unit. This allows for all rooms to have different thermostats so everyone can be comfortable, no matter what temperature they prefer.</p>
<p>Additionally, the split system offers more energy efficiency than ducted systems.
