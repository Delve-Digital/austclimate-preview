### Evaporative Cooling in Toorak

Hot, dry summer days don’t necessarily mean you have to shut all the windows to have your air conditioner on. As evaporative cooling requires doors and windows to be open, it’s ideal for alfresco living. You can keep the entrance to your outdoor entertaining area open while enjoying fresh, cooled air.

Evaporative cooling also brings several more benefits, including:

- A steady stream of fresh, cool air that doesn’t feel dry

- Lower energy consumption and greater cost-effectiveness

- An eco-friendly cooling solution that doesn’t use refrigerants
