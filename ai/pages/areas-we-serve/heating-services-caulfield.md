### Your Guide to Optimising the Performance of Your Ducted Heating System

Keeping your ducted heating system in top-notch condition is important not just for maintaining a comfortable indoor climate but also for ensuring the system's efficiency and lifespan. Here are some tips:

- Regular Servicing: Just like any other machine, your ducted heating system requires regular maintenance to function optimally. It's recommended to get your system serviced at least once a year, preferably before the onset of winter.

- Clean Filters: Clogged filters in your heating system can hamper its efficiency and even cause damage to the unit. Make sure to regularly clean or replace the filters as needed.

- Don't Overheat: Aim to keep your thermostat at a warm yet not overly toasty temperature. This practice can significantly save your energy usage over time.
