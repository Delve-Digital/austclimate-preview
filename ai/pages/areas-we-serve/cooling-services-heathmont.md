### Evaporative Cooling for Whole-Home Comfort

Evaporative cooling in Heathmont is a cost-effective and energy-efficient way to cool an entire home. These systems draw warm outside air through water-saturated cooling pads, reducing the temperature naturally before distributing it through ducts.

Because evaporative systems rely on fresh air rather than recirculating indoor air, they create a well-ventilated environment that many households prefer. They are also typically cheaper to run compared to refrigerated systems, particularly during extended periods of heat.

Australian Climate Solutions provides full evaporative cooling services, including installation, repairs, and maintenance. Regular servicing ensures the system remains clean and efficient, especially after exposure to dust and outdoor debris.

### Split Systems for Flexible Cooling

Split systems that Heathmont residents install are a popular option for targeted cooling. These systems consist of an indoor unit paired with an outdoor compressor and are ideal for cooling specific rooms or areas of the home.

They offer flexibility, energy efficiency, and relatively simple installation, making them well-suited to bedrooms, living spaces, and smaller properties. Split systems are particularly useful when only certain areas require cooling, helping to reduce overall energy consumption.

Australian Climate Solutions supplies and installs a wide range of split systems, ensuring each unit is properly sized and positioned for optimal performance. We also provide repairs and maintenance to keep your system running smoothly.

### Ducted and Add-On Cooling Systems

For homeowners seeking whole-home cooling, ducted systems provide consistent temperature control across multiple rooms. Where existing ducted heating is already in place, add-on cooling systems can integrate with the current infrastructure, offering an efficient way to upgrade without major changes.

These systems distribute cooled air evenly throughout the home, making them ideal for larger properties or households looking for a seamless climate control solution. They are designed to operate quietly and effectively, maintaining comfort even during peak summer conditions.

Our team assesses your current setup and recommends the most suitable ducted or add-on cooling solution. From installation to ongoing servicing, we ensure your system delivers reliable, long-term performance.
