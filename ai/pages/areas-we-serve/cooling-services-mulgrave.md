### Evaporative Cooling: Cool, Fresh, Affordable

Looking for a way to cool your home without drying out the air? [Evaporative cooling](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/cooling/evaporative-cooling-systems.md) is for you. These systems use evaporation to cool down fresh air from outside before circulating it throughout your home. Evaporative coolers are also perfect if you prefer to leave your windows open and enjoy the fresh air, minus the heat.

### Ducted Refrigerated Air Conditioning: The Ultimate Cooling Solution

[Refrigerated air conditioning](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/cooling/refrigerated-cooling.md) offers the ultimate in comfort, even on the hottest, most humid of days. Benefits of ducted refrigerated air conditioning include:

- Total Climate Control: Refrigerated systems let you set the temperature you want, regardless of the conditions outside – making them perfect for those humid days!

- Whole-Home Cooling: Ducted refrigerated systems are designed to cool every corner of your home. Plus, many systems incorporate zoning technology to tailor the temperature in specific rooms.

- Clean, Filtered Air: Many ducted refrigerated air conditioners are built with air filtering technology for a clean and healthy home or office.
