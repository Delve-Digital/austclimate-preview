### Split Systems

Split systems feature a wall-mounted indoor unit connected to an outdoor unit, and are great for custom climate control in one specific room. Multi-head split systems are also available, which cover more than one room with just the one outdoor unit.

A split system may be for you if:

- You’ve recently built an extension that your current ducted system doesn’t cover

- You only need to heat or cool certain rooms, not your whole home

- You’re after a solution that’s quicker and easier to install without complicated ductwork

- You have an open-plan home and want to focus on heating or cooling your main living area

### Evaporative Cooling

Evaporative cooling systems take in fresh air from outside, which then passes over water-soaked pads to cool your home via the process of evaporation. This is ideal for alfresco entertaining as well as for families with children or pets constantly entering and exiting the home.

At Australian Climate Systems, we supply evaporative cooling systems from quality brands like Braemar, Breezair, and Coolair.

Contact us today to install evaporative cooling in Park Orchards!
