### How to Maximise Your Air Conditioning System’s Efficiency

To get the most out of your air conditioning system in Seville, here are some useful tips:

- Maintenance: Regular servicing is key to maintaining the efficiency of your air conditioning system. Clean or replace filters regularly and have your system professionally serviced annually.

- Seal Leaks: Any leaks or drafts in your room can affect the efficiency of your air conditioning. Ensure windows, doors, and any gaps are properly sealed.

- Consider Location: The location of your outdoor unit is crucial. It should be installed in a shaded area, protected from direct sunlight and away from areas of dust or vegetation.

- Set Moderate Temperatures: Setting your thermostat correctly is key. Rather than setting your system to the lowest temperature, maintain a steady temperature that is comfortable for you and your family. A jump of every degree can use about 5% more energy.

- Book a Professional Service: Having your air conditioner in Seville serviced by experienced professionals ensures optimal functioning.
