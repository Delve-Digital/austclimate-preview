### Installation, Efficiency and Long-Term Performance

The best air conditioning installation for Chadstone isn't necessarily the highest-capacity system. Oversizing can affect cycling and efficiency, while undersizing can leave a system running continuously during more extreme weather. Regular servicing also helps protect long-term performance. Filters should be kept clean, outdoor units kept clear of obstructions, and the system checked periodically for airflow, drainage, electrical and mechanical problems.
