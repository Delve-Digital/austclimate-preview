### Refrigerated Cooling and Heating in Boronia

Refrigerated systems use refrigerant to cool the warm air inside your home, with the heat being released outdoors. In cool weather, the process can be reversed to heat your home.

This type of system is suited for older homes made from:

- Brick Veneer: Older brick veneer homes absorb and release external heat throughout the day, resulting in fluctuating indoor temperatures.

- Cladding: Cladding homes generally offer little in the way of insulation, so they heat up quickly in summer and lose heat quickly in winter.

One of the best types of air conditioning for Boronia’s older homes is refrigerated cooling and heating with inverter technology.

### What is Inverter Technology?

Air conditioners with inverter technology continuously adjust their output to meet the cooling or heating needs of the room, rather than remaining at one speed. This takes fluctuating temperatures into account and prevents wasting excess energy, helping you save on your power bill as well.

### Split Systems in Boronia

Split systems consist of an indoor and outdoor unit. The indoor unit circulates cool air into the room, while the outdoor unit releases the heat extracted from inside your home. In winter, this is reversed to keep the room cosy.

A split system is ideal for:

- Older cladding homes that may not have the roof space to install ducted air conditioning

- Anyone looking for budget-friendly air conditioning options, or who want to prioritise heating or cooling one specific room rather than the whole house

- An extension that’s not covered by the home’s existing ductwork

- Those looking for suitable air conditioning for a Boronia apartment or townhouse

### When Should You Service Your Air Conditioner?

At Australian Climate systems, we recommend servicing your air conditioner at least every 2–3 years. However, depending on how old your system is and how frequently you use it, you may need to service it more often.

You should book a service if you notice:

- Leaking, strange noises, or odours from your air conditioning unit

- Your air conditioner is not working as effectively as it used to

- Your energy bills have unexpectedly increased
