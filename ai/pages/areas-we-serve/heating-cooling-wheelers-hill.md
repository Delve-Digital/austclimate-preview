### Ducted Evaporative Cooling

Get an [evaporative central cooling](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/cooling/evaporative-cooling-systems.md) system for a comfortable Summer heat solution. Evap cooling works by replacing old, stale air with fresh, clean air. Other than its efficient way of cooling a space, evap air-conditioners are also great for hay fever or asthma sufferers. The way it filters out old air means unwanted dust particles don’t linger in the home or office.

### Hi-Wall Split System

Enjoy quiet and simple [air-conditioning](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/cooling/air-conditioning.md) with a hi-wall [split system](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/heating/split-systems.md). This is an easy to control and install cooling solution that suits any room at home or in the office. Australian Climate Systems works with trustworthy brands of hi-wall split systems. Some of these brands include ActronAir, [Daikin](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/brands/daikin.md) and [Panasonic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/brands/panasonic.md), who guarantee quality air-conditioning for personal or business use.

### Multi Split System

A multi split system allows you to power multiple cooling systems with one external unit. This powerful air-conditioning system means no one in the office or home misses out. Keep every room cool with a multi split system that doesn’t take up too much space outdoors with its single efficient unit.

### Ducted Refrigerated Systems

A ducted refrigerated system works by circulating air through ducts installed into your home or workspace. A [refrigerated system](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/cooling/refrigerated-cooling.md) is a central cooling solution that gives you complete control over the temperature of your whole household or office. This central cooling system also has zoning where you can have different temperatures going on throughout the building.

### Add-On Cooling

Want the luxury of having a refrigerated cooling system with your current gas ducted air-conditioner? Add-on cooling allows you to use your pre-installed heating system as a way to keep cool during the heat. This means you have year round comfort, without having to compromise your current gas ducted heating system.

### Gas Ducted Central Heating Services

Get your office space or house ready for Winter with central ducted heating. Gas ducted heaters give great temperature control over your whole household or office. Gas central heating is also an energy efficient heating solution. Save money and stay cosy this Winter with gas ducted central heating.

### Gas Log Fire

Gas log fires are a charming heating solution for your home or workspace. These stylish heating systems replicate the rustic appeal of fireplaces without all the real hassles. Each unit is either built into the wall or externally fitted to suit any interior design.

### Gas Space Heating

Gas space heaters are a versatile and robust heating solution. A gas space heater is portable and comes in different sizes to suit any room at home or in the office. Because of its use of gas instead of electricity a gas space heater is also energy efficient and eco-friendly.

Australian Climate Systems work with a broad range of space heaters including the Braemar range. Braemar space heaters include units with a 4.8 energy star rating so you can save money while keeping warm during Winter.

### Hydronic Heating

Hydronic heating solutions are a great option for those after a modern and eco-friendly heater. Unlike gas or electric heaters, a hydronic system uses boiled water to bring warmth in a room. Because hydronic heaters don’t rely on air circulation, it is also ideal for airborne allergy sufferers.

### Outdoor Heating

Australian Climate Systems aren’t limited to just indoor heating. We have outdoor heat strips to keep you warm outside during Winter. A heat strip is a ceiling installation for personal or business use. Warm up at home on your patio or balcony.

Or have heat strips for smoking areas, sidewalk cafes and pubs or alfresco dining.

### Electric Panel Heating

An electric panel system is an efficient heating solution for your home or office. An electric panel heater is a lightweight and compact unit that is fixed to a wall or can be portable. Because of its small size, it suits spaces that can’t afford to fit big, bulky heating systems.
