### Ducted Heating in Canterbury

A reliable heating solution is essential on those overcast autumn and winter days when you just can’t get warm. Ducted heating distributes heat quickly and evenly throughout your whole home, making it a more comfortable solution than a standalone heater that leaves cold spots.

### Best Air Conditioning for Luxury Open-Plan Homes

If you’re thinking of renovating your older Canterbury home and incorporating a spacious open-plan layout, the right air conditioning will ensure consistent heating and cooling across the entire space.

Split Systems: A high-capacity split system that can evenly heat or cool large zones allows you greater control over your indoor climate. At Australian Climate Systems, we supply and install split systems from renowned brands like Daikin, Panasonic, and LG. Contact us to install a split system in Canterbury today!

Zoned Ducted Systems: A zoned ducted air conditioning system will evenly distribute warm or cool air across larger zones through discreet ceiling vents. Ideally, choose one with inverter technology, which adjusts its output according to what the room needs. This results in consistent temperatures and improved energy efficiency.

### How to Keep Your Air Conditioner Working Efficiently

Ensuring your air conditioner is running as efficiently as possible benefits both the environment and your energy bill. We recommend:

- Seeking advice from experts to choose the right kind of air conditioning for your Canterbury home

- Reducing the amount of sunlight that gets into your home during summer with curtains, blinds, or window awnings

- Choosing an air conditioner with inverter technology; this is especially useful for older brick or cladding Canterbury homes where temperatures can fluctuate

- Heating or cooling only the rooms that need it; split systems and ducted air conditioning with zoning functionality is ideal for this purpose

- Scheduling a service every 2–3 years, or more frequently if required, to ensure all components of your air conditioner are in good working order
