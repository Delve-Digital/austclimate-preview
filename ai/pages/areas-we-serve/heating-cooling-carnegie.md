Homes and businesses don’t have to miss out on cooling systems when they have a ducted gas heater. Add-on cooling is an efficient solution for those who want year-round comfort. And [add-on air-conditioner](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/cooling/add-on-air-condition.md) will use the same ducts as your gas heater so it can give you refrigerated cooling. Therefore, you’re able to continue using your ducted gas heater during winter and enjoy cool air-conditioning during summer with add-on cooling.

### Gas Space Heating

Gas space heaters are a simple solution for those looking for convenient ways to stay warm during winter. A gas space heater is a standalone unit. It requires little to no installation, so you can enjoy warmth in your home instantly.

### Hydronic Heating

Hydronic heaters are an efficient modern solution to keep you warm this winter. To keep you cosy, a hydronic heater boils water in its panels or slabs to heat up a room. Because they don’t rely on cycling air, [hydronic heaters](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/ai/pages/heating/hydronic-heating.md) are ideal for asthma and hayfever sufferers.

Electric panels are a space-saving heating solution. The slim panel efficiently warms up small spaces without taking up too much space. Because of its compact system, an electric panel heater is lightweight, low-maintenance and energy-efficient.
