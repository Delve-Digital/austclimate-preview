### What Kind of Air Conditioning is Best for You?

The right air conditioning for your home in Rowville depends on the size of your house, the material it’s constructed from, and how you use your space.

Zoned Ducted Heating and Cooling: If you’re thinking of updating your home’s heating and cooling or planning a knockdown-rebuild, consider installing ducted air conditioning. It’s one of the most effective solutions for larger family homes.

Is Ducted Air Conditioning Energy-Efficient? Many modern ducted systems are designed to be highly energy-efficient.

- Zoning: Zoned systems let you personalise the temperature in each room, or only heat/cool the rooms you’re currently using.

- Inverter Technology: Modern systems adjust their energy output depending on the needs of the room. This is especially good for older brick veneer or cladding homes.

Split Systems: Split systems are great for extensions and individual rooms. They’re energy-efficient, affordable, and easy to install, and allow you to customise the temperature in one specific room. Contact us to install a split system in Rowville today!

### Evaporative Cooling

As Rowville is more inland, summers tend to be hot and dry. This makes evaporative cooling an excellent choice for staying comfortable in the summer months.

Benefits of evaporative cooling in Rowville:

- Evaporative cooling is affordable and energy-efficient, using minimal electricity to run.

- As it uses the process of evaporation to cool down fresh air from outside, the resulting air will feel more comfortable and less dry than some other systems.

- You can leave your doors and windows open. This is great for homes with kids and pets who are constantly running in and out, or for when you’re entertaining on your alfresco. No more wasting cool air every time someone opens a door!

- Some homeowners install evaporative cooling in addition to their current refrigerated system. The evaporative cooling can be used on hotter, drier days and help you cut down on your power bill, whereas the refrigerated system can be used on more humid days or for heating during winter.
