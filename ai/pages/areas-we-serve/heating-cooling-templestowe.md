### Heating a Larger Home Efficiently

Many established houses have traditionally relied on ducted gas heating. Where the equipment is older, problems may not be limited to the heater itself. Flexible ductwork can deteriorate in roof spaces, connections can loosen, and inadequate insulation can allow heated air to escape before reaching the rooms that need it most.

Ducted heating Templestowe homeowners already have should therefore be assessed as a complete system before considering replacement. Checking the heater, thermostat, return-air path, ductwork and airflow can reveal whether a relatively straightforward repair will restore performance or whether replacement is justified.

For larger properties, zoning is particularly valuable. It allows sleeping areas, living spaces and unused rooms to be treated differently rather than heating the entire house whenever one part needs attention.

### Cooling Open Living Areas and Upstairs Rooms

Summer can expose weaknesses that are less noticeable during winter. Large windows, west-facing rooms, open-plan renovations and upper-level bedrooms can accumulate considerable heat during Melbourne's hotter periods.

Split systems Templestowe homeowners choose can work well when cooling is needed in specific rooms without conditioning the entire property. For a substantial family home, a zoned refrigerated system may be more appropriate where several living and sleeping areas need to be controlled independently.

Evaporative cooling for Templestowe properties may also suit where occupants want whole-house airflow and are comfortable with the system's different operating characteristics. The home's layout, window use and insulation should be considered before choosing evaporative cooling over refrigerated equipment.

### Knowing When a Repair is Enough

Weak airflow, strange noises, water around indoor units, frequent cycling or rooms failing to reach the desired temperature all warrant investigation. Common causes include blocked filters, dirty coils, failing fans, electrical faults, restricted airflow and damaged ductwork.

A proper inspection should establish the source of the fault before parts are replaced. If an older unit has become unreliable, requires repeated repairs or relies on obsolete components, replacing it may provide better long-term value.

For a new system, air conditioning installation in Templestowe properties requires more than calculating floor area. Insulation, glazing, ceiling height, room orientation, occupancy and the relationship between existing and new sections of the house can all affect the required capacity.
