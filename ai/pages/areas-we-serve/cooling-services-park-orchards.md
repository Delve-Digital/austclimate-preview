### Informational Insights: How to Extend the Life of Your Heating and Cooling System

Just like your other appliances and equipment, your heating and cooling systems benefit significantly from regular servicing and maintenance as well as thoughtful use.

- Don’t Overwork Your System: Overworking your system can reduce its life expectancy. Use your air conditioning only when necessary.

- Set Moderate Temperatures: Setting your temperatures to moderately cool or warm depending on the weather can promote the longevity of your system.

- Schedule Periodic Maintenance: Schedule professional inspections, servicing, and cleanings periodically, not just when your system fails. This prevents minor issues from escalating into significant and costly problems.
