## Are Air Conditioning Brands Actually Important?

Just like most things in life, the brand of your air conditioner can determine a lot about your system. Choosing a high-quality, reliable brand will ensure your system is more cost-effective and energy efficient. It also gives you the peace of mind that you can keep cool when it matters most! While these systems can sometimes be in a more expensive price range, they're bound to save you money in the long term thanks to their low power consumption and excellent performance.

## Looking for Add-On Air Conditioning? ActronAir Could Be the Option for You!

If you've already got a gas ducted system and you want to invest in air conditioning, why not simply add it on?

Add-on air conditioning can often piggyback off your existing gas ducted heating architecture, meaning you can use the same ducts to heat and cool your home, just like with reverse cycle! ActronAir offers add-on ducted air conditioning systems that are designed for Aussie conditions and will work just as well as any standalone air conditioner. 

Brivis is another good option when it comes to add-on refrigerated air conditioning. If you already have Brivis for your gas ducted heating, there's a good chance your system can easily be upgraded to add Brivis refrigerated air conditioning. Brivis call this DualComfort, and it's a great way to invest in both heating and cooling, whether your home was built on stumps or a slab.

## How to Select the Right Air Conditioning Brand?

Still unsure about the best air conditioner brands? If you want to know how to buy the best air conditioner, simply talk to an expert supplier and installer from a trusted Australian company. Asking the right questions can help you narrow down your options, ensuring you land on a system that prioritises what's important to you.

Some things to ask about when shopping for an air conditioner include:

- Your price range and finding an air conditioning brand that meets your budget: Remember to consider both the upfront installation cost and ongoing running costs 

- Determining the right size air conditioner for your home: An expert can recommend the best size air conditioner for larger spaces, smaller rooms, and whole-home cooling. Installers can also help you find a system when you have limited floor and ceiling space, such as a bulkhead system

- How quiet you need your air conditioner to be: Most of the air conditioner brands mentioned in this article are modern and quiet, but an expert can give you specific advice if a whisper-quiet system is your top priority

- System placement and ductwork installation: If your outdoor unit or indoor unit is in the wrong position, you won't enjoy maximum performance or efficiency. Similarly, poorly installed ductwork will negatively impact your system, regardless of brand!

- Heating and Cooling: If you want combined heating and cooling, you can opt for a reverse cycle system or gas heating with add-on air conditioning. To find out which option is best for you, talk to a professional.
