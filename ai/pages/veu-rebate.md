## What is the
VEU rebate?

The Victorian Energy Upgrades program supports eligible energy-efficient upgrades through discounts arranged with an accredited provider.

Eligible heating and cooling upgrades use approved high-efficiency reverse-cycle systems, either ducted or non-ducted. The system being replaced must be more than two years old.

Arrange the upgrade with an accredited provider or their partner installer before buying a product or starting work. Retrospective discounts are generally not available. The final price varies by installation and provider.

## VEU rebate
questions.

How much is the VEU air conditioning rebate?

There is no single amount for every home. The existing equipment, proposed product, upgrade type and provider pricing affect the discount. Request a written quote for your specific installation.

Can I get a free air conditioner?

Do not assume an upgrade is free. Confirm the complete installed price, the eligible discount and any additional work before accepting an offer.

Can a split system or ducted system qualify?

Eligible reverse-cycle products may be available in both categories. The product and installation must meet the program rules; our team can discuss the options for your property.

Can I replace gas heating with electric heating and cooling?

Discuss your current gas heater and your preferred electric system with our team. Confirm eligibility and any required decommissioning before booking.
