## Advanced Technology Features

What does a Mitsubishi Electric split system offer in terms of technology?

#### The full inverter compressor technology

This allows you to adjust the speed and power of your air conditioning unit based on the temperature you want for your spaces. 

For Mitsubishi Electric split systems, this outdoor unit DC scroll compressor (with the addition of a frame compliance mechanism) reduces the compressor's internal friction, increasing efficiency even in larger spaces.

#### I-Save Mode

A Mitsubishi Electric split system includes an I-Save Mode, which reduces power consumption. This simple setting function remembers your preferred (pre-set) temperature at the press of a button on the remote controller.

#### Quiet Mode 

This setting is added to the fan speed settings, giving you a super quiet operation as low as 18dB, which is perfect for your bedroom. You'll barely know it's turned on!

#### 3D I-See sensor

![Top Reasons to Choose a Mitsubishi Electric Split System for Your Home](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/media/f329b9bfc3b3-3D-I-See-sensor.webp)

This infra-red sensor includes eight vertically arranged sensor elements that analyse the room temperature in three dimensions.

The system can judge when people are present in the room, creating ‘indirect airflow’ to avoid airflow hitting people directly and ‘direct airflow’ to deliver airflow to where people are. When cooling your room, the middle of the room is detected to be warmer, and more airflow is directed towards it.

#### Night Mode 

This feature reduces the brightness of the operation indicator light, disables beeping noises, and limits the outdoor unit's noise level.

#### Free piping reuse technology

Mitsubishi Electric has a clean, free piping reuse technology, so you can reuse an existing refrigerant pipe and cut installation costs. The system is fitted with a 'wide strainer' that captures iron particles and prevents them from entering the outdoor unit.

Your Mitsubishi Electric will never use more power than it needs to, so you don't need to worry about a sudden influx in your energy bill.

## Flexibility in Configurations

Ranging from wall-mounted to freestanding, ceiling cassette or ceiling suspended, Mitsubishi Electric split systems indoor unit offers true flexibility with a wide range of options to satisfy most application requirements.

Mitsubishi Electric systems have a vertical and horizontal vane design, so you can easily adjust the airflow direction and improve airflow. There is also a long airflow configuration, a high-power mode that combines with the long mode to push air further out.

Cold or warm air won't be contained in one small area but in every part of your space.

For example, the[ MSZ-AP Split System Air Conditioner](https://www.mitsubishielectric.com.au/product/msz-ap25-80vgd-split-system-air-conditioner/) is easy to operate, has clear instructions and will heat rooms even when all internal doors are open in your home.

Mitsubishi Electric split systems include advanced filtration systems such as the Plasma Quad Connect Air Purifying Filter. 

This optional filter connects to your air conditioner and improves air quality by inhibiting key pollutants such as Influenza, viruses, bacteria, PM2.5, allergens, dust, and mould. Experience a healthier environment, reassurance, and peace of mind at home.

These split systems also have Dual Barrier Coating, which prevents dust, greasy dirt, or airborne contaminants from collecting on the air conditioner's surface. Dirt is classified into two groups: hydrophilic dirt, such as fibre dust, and hydrophobic dirt, such as cigarette smoke.

This barrier coating has blended “fluorine particles” that stop this penetration, keeping your aircon clean.
