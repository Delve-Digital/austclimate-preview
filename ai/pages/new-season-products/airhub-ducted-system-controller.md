## Sensor Options

It's the unique features of Daikin products that makes us the world leader.

- Touch Operation -  7” colour resistive touch screen interface housed in a contemporary casing design with a matte white finish.

- Two Versions -  Both On/Off or Linear Control options available in either a 4 or 8 zone design. For details on these control methods, see brochure.

- Optional Wireless Sensors -  Wireless remote temperature sensors are available, ideal for homes with internal brick walls.

- On/Off Timer -  Program on and off times within the day to suit your needs.

- Weekly Schedule Timer -  Program on and off times, including when to open/close zones and the temperature sensor to use for On/Off Control option.

- Eco Settings -  Setpoint Range Limit, Setpoint Auto Reset and Auto Off Timer enables you to easily reduce your system's energy consumption.

Available in two options:
1. Zone Control (On/Off) - only compatible with FDYQ(N) and FDYA(N) 

- Each zone can be turned ON or OFF

- Fan speed can be selected from H/M/L/Auto and Airside*

- Airside mode - fan RPM is regulated between 60-100% of nominal AFR affording to the number of active zones

- Up to 2 optional remote temperature sensors can be connected for RA sensing

*Airside Control is only available on FDYQ & FDYA(N) models only
2. Individual Room Control (Linear) - only compatible with FDYA(N) 

- Each zone can be turned ON or OFF with temperature selection +2ºC from the main setpoint

- Dampers automatically adjust opening (linearly in 5º increments) to deliver AFR to maintain zone temperature

- Fan speed is locked in OptiZone mode - fan RPM is automatically regulated between 30-100% of nominal AFR to maintain constant fan ESP

- Each zone must have a remote temperature sensor for zone temperature sensing

- Common Zone is not required
Features applicable to both options: 

- Optional wireless remote temperature sensors

- On/Off Timer & 7 Day Timer with advanced functionality such as Zone Control

- Compatible with Daikin Airbase (Wi-Fi adapter) featuring Amazon Alexa compatibility for voice control.
