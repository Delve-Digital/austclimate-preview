## Air-e (Ceiling Mount nanoe™X Generator)

This product is the first of its kind and brings all the benefits of nanoe X active air purification together as a standalone product. Approved by the National Asthma Council of Australia’s Sensitive Choice Program, air-e delivers a range of benefits including.

- Deodorising odours

- Inhibiting bacteria and viruses

- Inhibiting mould, pollen and other allergens

- Moisturising skin and hair.

Air-e can be used in a range of applications including:

- Homes

- Aged care

- Education

- Hospitals

- Restaurants
