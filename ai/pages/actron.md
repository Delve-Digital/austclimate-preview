## Why choose a Tru-Inverter?

Tru-Inverter is more efficient, running at as low as 20% for improved efficiency when conventional systems can only run as little as 40%. It can also heat and cool your home a phenomenal 5 times faster than conventional systems.

## Why ‘capacity’ can be an ‘air con’ job

Some systems cannot run at full capacity constantly. This is not good on a 40 degree day. ESP Platinum with TruMax functionality can run at top capacity day and night.
