## High-quality makes

Daikin air conditioners are Aussie made! They have dedicated over 90 years to developing technologies that make heating and cooling your home or business effective. Daikin meets quality and safety standards with an Australian-made certification, which gives you peace of mind. You’ll always get a reliable product.

Manufacturing operations at Daikin are based on a zero-defect policy as well, so production line workers must carry out strict controls and tests.

Daikin has also obtained the globally recognised certification ISO 9001 Quality Management System, and ISO 14001 for environmental performance.

![Top Reasons Why Daikin Air Conditioning Is a Smart Investment](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/media/db8231efa09d-Zena-Daikin-model.webp)

The [Zena](https://www.daikin.com.au/our-product-range/split-system-air-conditioning/zena) Daikin model is an ultra compact design, a discrete casing size of only 295mm high, 798mm wide and 185mm deep. There is a White Hair Line option as well as the Black Wood shade. This model also has vertical airflow during heating operation, the discharge louvres streaming airflow down the wall and towards the floor for better comfort and distribution of temperature.

02
## Many configurations

In terms of the split system, the setup is what gives a Daikin air conditioner its name. Depending on your needs, you can choose from many configurations such as:

- > Wall mounted models that are discreet and efficient.

- > Compact floor consoles ideal for limited spaces (or if you’re just wanting to heat/cool one room)

- > Ceiling units for air circulation.

To warm or cool down multiple rooms, Daikin has multi split systems that allow multiple door units to connect to a single outdoor condenser. This air conditioning option cuts back installation costs and space.

03
## They’re energy efficient

Daikin systems are energy efficient; Daikin as a whole does their part in being environmentally conscious. All meet the Australian Minimum Energy Performance Standards (ANPS). The Cora model, for example:

- > When you push the POWERFUL Button on the remote control, it gives you a boost in heating or cooling for a 20 minute period, even if the unit is already operating at full capacity.

- > Has an R32 Refrigerant, enabling increased energy efficiency and significantly reducing the Global Warming Potential Factor.

- > Includes precision control, so temperatures can be set in increments of 0.5 to ensure optimal comfort conditions throughout the year.

![Top Reasons Why Daikin Air Conditioning Is a Smart Investment](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/media/b79e98f4d142-smart-technology.webp)

Systems have smart technology, such as air filtration and purification, to improve the comfort levels for your family. Daikin is great because it provides an extra layer of protection against frustrating allergens. Experience advanced filtration and humidity control with:

- > A titanium apatite deodorising filter. This traps microscopic airborne particles while decomposing odours.

- > An enzyme blue deodorant filter which absorbs and deactivates bacteria and viruses.

- > A program dry function which controls humidity levels while maintaining room temperature as well. This system automatically adjusts airflow rates and cooling settings to prevent moisture build-up.

Split system models are approved by the National Asthma Foundation Australia’s Sensitive Choice Program and carry the blue butterfly symbol. So, if you have asthma or allergies such as dust or pollen, a Daikin system is worth the money.

For example, the Alira X model has:

- > Three layers of filtration, prefilter for larger particulates, enzyme blue deodorising filter for odours and Daikin’s Streamer Technology for a powerful oxidative decomposition of pollutants

- > An intelligent eye. This work was an infrared human presence sensor that has energy-saving operations when the room is unoccupied for 20 minutes.

- > Coanda airflow discharge louvres are designed to stream air along the ceiling for longer throws and deliver rapid cooling and even distribution of temperature.

- > Built-in Wi-Fi for connection to Daikin’s Mobile Controller smartphone interface. It’s conveniently included inside the casing of the indoor unit.

## A diverse range of air conditioning

Split system air conditioning to heat or cool a single room. A variety of models are available to suit your requirements and can be bought in either wall mounted or floor standing types. The Alira X model is good for heating or cooling small, medium and large spaces and maintains room ambience.

Multi split air conditioning can heat or cool multiple rooms with one outdoor unit. Each indoor unit can be individually controlled, scheduled and set to the desired room temperature. The VRV IV-S model is best for heating or cooling up to 14 rooms, small block sized with limited outdoor space, or larger luxury homes.

The Super Multi NX is great for up to 5 rooms, has quiet multiple room air conditioning, and individual room control as you see fit.

Ducted air conditioning can heat or cool your entire home! It can be installed in a new home or tailored to suit an existing one. Once installed, the return air grilles are visible inside your property. The Premium Inverter Ducted model heats and cools the whole home and suits small or large houses.

It’s also best for retrofitting an old R22 ducted system.

## Long lifespan and warranty options

Daikin systems last around 20 years on average, and if there’s ever an issue with them, there’s a warranty guarantee. For example, Daikin compressors are optimised to work efficiently in all environments.

A 5-year parts and labour warranty applies to split, multi split and ducted air conditioners purchased and installed in homes across Australia. When you choose Daikin, you do so with the confidence of knowing you have many years of worry-free comfort ahead.

Along with a domestic unit warranty, Daiking provides them for commercial units and air purifier ranges.
