## Common Air Conditioner Issues and How To Troubleshoot

So, your Panasonic air conditioner has suddenly shut off, and you’re worried you won’t be able to cool your bedroom before the day's heat kicks in. Let’s dive into the most common problems and what you can do to fix them.

02
## Your Panasonic AC isn't turning on

- > Your air conditioner refusing to start is always frustrating, especially if you're starting to feel the heat on a hot day!

- > Make sure to check your fuse box to ensure it hasn't blown

- > Is the air conditioning system plugged in?

- > Change or check the batteries in your remote controller

- > Always check to see if a timer has been set for your system to turn off

Are these steps not working? Consider resetting the system

All you need to do is flip the AC's circuit breaker inside your fuse box and wait around a minute before attempting to turn it back on. If you're still noticing issues after this, you should reach out to a professional for assistance.

03
## Your Panasonic AC isn't heating or cooling

- > Is the mode of your system set to the correct one, such as cool or heat?

- > Check your set temperature and if it is low enough for cooling to occur or high enough for heating

- > Although this seems like an obvious thing to do, ensure that your windows and doors are shut

- > Do you have dirty air filters? Unclean filters can make it more difficult for the AC to propel cool or warm air into the room.

- > Your air inlets and outlets may be obstructed; for example, your outdoor unit shouldn't have anything surrounding it, as this can affect its ability to expel hot or cool air. Always check that nothing is blocking the flow of air from the system

04
## Your Panasonic AC is leaking

Noticing a considerable amount of leaking? You will want to call a professional as soon as possible to help.

Small drops of water are common for any AC system, but larger amounts can be caused by clogged drain pans, rust drain pans, disconnected drain lines, and more. When in doubt, never attempt more complex troubleshooting issues yourself.

05
## Your Panasonic AC remote control isn't working

- > You might need to change the batteries

- > Make sure that your batteries are installed correctly by resetting the remote by using a pin to push the RC button on the bottom right

- > When pushing a button on your remote, see if the LED sensor light activates

- > If nothing activates, the underlying problem could be the sensor wire inside the remote; in this case, a replacement remote is a cheaper option

Does the Panasonic AC remote control work intermittently, or does it have a faded display? Your batteries are probably dying, so replacing them can fix this issue.

06
## Your Panasonic AC smells strange

How can you fix the two common Panasonic air con smells?

- > Musty smells are caused by bacteria build-up or mould inside your AC or dirty filters.

- > Rotten egg smells can mean there's a dead animal inside your unit (such as a mouse); although you can check the easy-to-reach areas of your system, you are most likely going to need to call a technician.

07
## Your Panasonic AC will not turn off

If your Panasonic AC doesn't turn off, you may have a faulty thermostat, a dirty condenser coil, or a damaged control board. Although you can identify some of these problems yourself, a heating and cooling technician will need to fix them. How can you know there is something wrong?

- > Temperature fluctuations in your room can be a clear sign of a faulty thermostat

- > Check your condenser coil to see if it's dirty by looking at the top part of your outdoor unit; if there is dirt or leaves, they must be cleaned out

Is your outdoor AC unit not working normally? Check to see if it's plugged in and if the circuit breaker is turned on in your fuse box. If all else fails, you need to call a technician.

08
## Your Panasonic AC fault codes

When the timer light flashes on your outdoor unit, there is a system error; however, it doesn't always show automatically on the unit or remotely. Here are some helpful steps to get the error code!

- > Push a pin into the check hole on your remote and hold for around 5 seconds until the screen turns black

- > Hold the up button until you hear a drawn-put beep. The fault code will show!

Although your air conditioner can still run after years of use, that doesn’t mean it’s cooling your space. You could have a number of issues with your system and not realise! So, why is it so critical to maintain your Panasonic AC?

You’ll be saving money, use a lot less energy, and experience better cooling capabilities.

- > Clogged filters. You don’t want blocked filters as this can cause sneezing, coughing, and stop your AC from cooling your area. It is recommended to change filters regularly and engage in professional inspections to avoid these problems.

- > Seasonal changes. It’s important to get your Panasonic AC ready for different weather conditions. For example, setting the temperature between 18 to 20 degrees Celsius supports energy efficiency and optimal performance. You can also use dry mode to reduce humidity during mild and wet winter days. 

- > Cutting back power bills. You don’t always want to put your AC on maximum coolness settings as it can use more power than it should. Using Dry Mode on humid days uses less energy.
