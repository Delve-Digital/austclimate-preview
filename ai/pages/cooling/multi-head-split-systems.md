Multi Head Split Systems

## Room-by-room comfort. One outdoor unit.

A multi-head system connects several indoor units to one outdoor unit. High-wall split systems in bedrooms and living areas let you choose which rooms to condition, with individual room controls.

For a three-bedroom home, we can assess indoor units for each bedroom and the main living areas. The number, capacity and combination of units depend on the selected system and the property.

- Individual heating and cooling control in each room.

- One outdoor unit serving multiple indoor units.

- A potentially more affordable alternative to whole-home ducted reverse cycle in suitable homes.

- Flexibility to condition the rooms you use.

When replacing gas central heating with electric heating and cooling, VEU rebates may be available, subject to property, system and program eligibility. Ask us to assess eligibility before you commit.

## Indoor options
to suit your rooms.

High-wall units are a practical starting point for bedrooms and living spaces. A compatible multi-head system can also use concealed bulkhead units, floor-mounted units or ceiling cassettes.

We will help you compare room space, appearance, installation access and compatible unit combinations. Indoor units connected to the same outdoor unit generally share heating or cooling mode; individual controls do not mean simultaneous heating and cooling.

## Concealed bulkhead. A discreet alternative.

Bulkhead systems conceal the indoor unit within a ceiling bulkhead, leaving a grille visible in the room. They can suit apartments, townhouses, renovations and new builds where the space and installation requirements are suitable.

We can work with builders and owner-builders before plastering to plan a discreet installation. Ask our team about compatible options.
