## What Is Refrigerated Cooling?

Refrigerated heating and cooling systems work by drawing in the warm air from your indoor environment. The refrigerant within the unit absorbs the heat from the air, and the cool, fresh air is circulated back into your home. 

The flexible nature of refrigerated cooling and heating systems has made them a popular choice in both residential and commercial spaces.

Benefits of refrigerated cooling systems include:
