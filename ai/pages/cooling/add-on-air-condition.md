## Brivis Dual Comfort

Brivis Dual Comfort combines compatible Brivis gas ducted heating with add-on refrigerated cooling. It provides gas heating in winter and refrigerated cooling in summer through a suitable shared duct and outlet layout.

Compatibility matters. The heater, ductwork, outlets, electrical supply and installation space need to be assessed before adding cooling.

[Download the Brivis system brochure (PDF, opens a new tab)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Delve-Digital/austclimate-preview/preview/media/9aeae26edbed-Brivis-GDH-Brochure.pdf). Discuss current models and compatibility with our team.
