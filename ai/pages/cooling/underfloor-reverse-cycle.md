## Replace gas heating with electric heating and cooling

Considering a change from gas ducted heating? Underfloor reverse cycle installation may be an option where your home has suitable subfloor access and space.

It can provide heating and cooling through a ducted system. A site assessment is needed to work out the right equipment, duct layout and outlet positions for your property.

## Start with a property assessment

Our team can inspect the available underfloor space, access, existing outlets and installation requirements.

Existing gas heating ducts and grilles are not automatically suitable for reverse cycle air conditioning. We will discuss what can be retained and what needs to change before recommending a system.

Replacing eligible gas heating with an eligible electric system may qualify for support through Victorian Energy Upgrades.

Eligibility, equipment requirements and available incentives need to be checked for your circumstances before quoting. No rebate amount or eligibility is guaranteed.

## Frequently asked
questions.

Can I keep my existing gas heating ducts?

Not always. Reverse cycle systems have different airflow and insulation requirements. Our team needs to assess the existing ducts and outlets before advising what can be reused.

Is every home suitable for an underfloor installation?

No. Subfloor clearance, safe access, drainage and the proposed duct layout all affect suitability. A site assessment will help identify practical options.

Can I receive a VEU rebate?

Eligibility depends on your existing heating, the replacement system and current program requirements. Ask our team to check the requirements for your property before you proceed.
