## Ducted Air Conditioning

Mitsubishi Electric ducted air conditioning is climate control at the touch of a button. It provides a central air conditioning solution to maintain total comfort throughout the whole home. Ducted air conditioning starts with an indoor unit subtly installed in the ceiling, circulating conditioned air throughout the home. The outdoor unit is installed neatly on the side of the house, where there is plenty of free space on either side to allow for airflow and easy access for maintenance.
