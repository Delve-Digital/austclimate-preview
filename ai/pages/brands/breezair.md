## Why Install a Breezair Evaporative Cooler?

An evaporative cooler is a simple, cost-effective way to keep your space feeling cool and fresh throughout the summer months. Breezair evaporative coolers offer a range of benefits, such as:

- Affordability: The low upfront cost and low electricity usage of Breezair evaporative coolers helps you save both short- and long-term.

- Fresh, Cool Air: Filtered air technology ensures a constant supply of cool, fresh air for your home even during the hottest part of the day.

- Sustainability: Breezair evaporative coolers are designed to emit less greenhouse gases as well as no CFCs or HFCs, making them an environmentally-conscious choice.
