2a:T449,<p>Generally speaking, all ducted heating systems work the same. Hot air is generated, distributed throughout the ducts in your roof or floor, and sent out the vents and into your home. This process is repeated until the desired temperature is reached.</p><p>However, there are a few differences in how a ducted gas heater works compared to a reverse cycle system.</p>
<p>Ducted gas heating draws in air from inside your home. This travels through the return air grille and over the heat exchanger. The air is heated via gas combustion and delivered throughout your home in the process described above.</p>
<p>Reverse cycle ducted heating systems don't generate heat in the same way that gas systems or other electric systems do. Instead, they absorb heat energy from the outdoor air and transfer this indoors to heat the home. This process involves coils, heat pumps, and refrigerant, and it will work even if it's really cold outside.</p>
