<p>Whether you’re always adjusting your only <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/split-systems/">split system</a> in the living room, or you're going around the house changing the temperature of every room, it can get exhausting not having a centralised cooling system.</p>
<p>The problem with central cooling systems is that everyone has to put up with the same temperature, and that could cause some heated arguments.</p>
<p>The solution is central cooling systems with zoning!</p>
<p>Air conditioning zoning gives you the power to set different temperatures and airflow settings throughout your home. This is the perfect way to combat Melbourne's unpredictable weather while meeting the different needs of everyone at home.</p>
<p>As experts in <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/air-conditioning/"><b><i>air conditioning in Melbourne</i></b></a><b><i>,</i></b> our team knows all about the power of zoning.</p>

Air conditioning zoning can be installed from the very beginning, or you can retrofit certain ducted air conditioning units with zoning technology.</p>
<p>Homeowners typically zone each living area and their bedrooms separately, but in some cases, you can create a separate zone for every room in your home.</p>
<p>Zoned air conditioning systems have many of the same components as standard ducted air conditioners, including:</p>
