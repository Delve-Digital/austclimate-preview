23:T420,<p>There's nothing better than rushing into your house to escape the Melbourne chill on a cold winter day. And there's nothing worse than realising that your heater isn't working so you've barely escaped the cold!</p>
<p>The kind of problems that your heater is having can range from minor to extreme. Your system might just need a little tweaking, or it might require professional repairs or replacements. Before you automatically assume the latter, check out our guide to some of the <strong><em>most common issues with heating systems and what might be causing them.</em></strong></p>
<p>Whether you have <a href="/austclimate-preview/areas-we-serve/heating-services-camberwell/"><strong><em>ducted heating in Camberwell</em></strong></a><strong><em>, </em></strong><a href="/austclimate-preview/areas-we-serve/hydronic-heating-lilydale/"><strong><em>hydronic heating in Lilydale</em></strong></a>, or another heating and cooling system, this guide is here to help you.</p>
<p>Uncover the cause of your heating troubles and take the next step today!</p>24:T452,<p>If you have noticed that your heater is contributing to the cold instead of warming up the room, the first thing to do is turn it off.</p>
<p>If you have a gas system like a <a href="/austclimate-preview/heating/ducted-gas-heating/"><strong>gas ducted heater</strong></a>, check to make sure your house hasn't had a gas interruption. <em>A lack of fuel is one of the most common causes of heating malfunctions of any kind.</em></p>
<p>After this, check air filters to see if there is a blockage or a collection of dust and debris. If so, try giving them a clean. Don't attempt to stick any foreign objects (including your fingers) into the vents or other internal parts of your heating system.</p>
<p>Other potential reasons your heater may be emitting cold air include damage to different parts of the system, such as the ductwork, the pilot light, a faulty heating element, or problems with other internal components.
