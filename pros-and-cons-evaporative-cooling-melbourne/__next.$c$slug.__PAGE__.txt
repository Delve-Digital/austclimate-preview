33:T4b1,<p>Before we dive into the pros and cons, let's explore the difference between evaporative cooling and other air conditioners.</p>
<p>Evaporative coolers are sometimes called evaporative air conditioners, but they're not really air conditioners at all. </p>
<p>Most<strong> <a href="/austclimate-preview/cooling/air-conditioning/">types of air conditioning</a> </strong>use refrigerant gas, pumps, and fans to absorb the air in your home, cool it down, and redistribute it. The windows and doors remain closed because you don't want the warm outdoor air mixing with the cool indoor air.</p>
<p>Evaporative cooling systems use fans, water pumps and cooling pads to cool outdoor air and distribute it throughout your home. Outdoor air is passed over water-soaked pads, which cools the air naturally through the process of evaporation. That refreshing breeze is then sent through the evaporative cooling ducts.</p><p>The windows and doors remain open because evaporative coolers constantly cycle new air throughout your home, rather than reusing the same air.</p>
<p>Evaporative coolers are sometimes shortened to Evap coolers, and in other parts of the world, they are called swamp coolers.</p>
