<h4><strong>Here are some further troubleshooting questions if you think your Brivis needs a repair.</strong></h4>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Why is water continually running while the unit is on?</strong> All Brivis AD models use the “bleed off” method for tank water quality control. This will rid the cooler of any salt or sediments and allow for the tank to be topped with clean water.</p>
<p><strong>Why does the unit dump water periodically during operation?</strong> Brivis Advance and Brivis Promina coolers use a “running refresh” method for tank water quality control. This ensures the salt or sediments accumulating in the cooler don’t exceed an acceptable level.</p>
<p><strong>There is no air coming out of some of the vents.</strong> It is probable that this is an installation issue. Contact your installer as soon as possible.</p>
<p><strong>Why does the air smell different?</strong> New cooling pads can give off a mild odour while they are settling in. It will dissipate quickly as the new pad is constantly flushed during use. In this case, the unit may need a service.</p>
<p><strong>What does duct cleaning look like? </strong>Remove dust from your cooling system, kill potential dust mites, remove odours from your cooling system, clean the cooler fan and assembly, and clean the supply air diffusers.</p>
<p><strong>Why doesn’t the cooler turn on when the current time and room temperature is displayed, yet the word “cooler” is not?</strong> Your Brivis cooler has lost communication with your Networker wall controller. A service call is required.</p>
<p><strong>How far should your cooler be away from sanitary vents?</strong> The unit should be at least a 5-metre radius away from a sanitary outlet.</p>
<p><strong>Should the unit have its own circuit in the meter box?</strong> Yes. We recommend that the power socket is wired back to the meter box on a dedicated power circuit.</p>
<p><strong>Why is there condensation and moisture on my tiles?</strong> Make sure the vents are directed across the room and not towards the floor. Sufficient doors and windows must be open, and if it is a humid day, turn the pump off.</p>
<p>Can you run heating through the same ductwork as the evaporative cooler? No. The different technology between ducted heating and evaporative cooling doesn’t allow the units to share the same ductwork. Evaporative cooling needs a larger ductwork for airflow.</p>
<h4><strong>What are the benefits of regularly servicing your Brivis evaporative cooler?</strong></h4>
<p></p>
<p><strong>The system will last a whole lot longer</strong>. A service works as a routine checkup, but it’s also investing in the future of your system. It will run for years to come.</p>
<p><strong>More energy efficien</strong>t. Even the best systems experience wear and tear! A service slices your energy consumption and bills, so you’re paying less, which benefits your bank account. When the components are functioning properly, the Brivis AC works at optimal efficiency.</p>
<p><strong>You won’t pay for as many repairs down the line</strong>. A random breakdown is stressful (and pricey if the AC needs to be fully replaced), so a service sorts these issues</p>
<p>Your cooler should be serviced at two-year intervals after the date of installation by a qualified, licensed technician. Failure to do so during the product warranty period may void your warranty.</p>
<p>For example, the Brivis controller has a spanner symbol which reminds you that it’s time for a service, or it might signal there’s an issue with your unit. Press the Fn button followed by key 1 on your controller.</p>
<p>If the display is reading “fan run hours indicate it’s time for a service call”, the unit is due for a service!
