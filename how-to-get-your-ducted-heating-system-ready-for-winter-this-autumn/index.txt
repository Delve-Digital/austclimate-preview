36:T41d,<p>Everyone wants to keep spaces warm and reduce heating expenses during winter, but how can it be done? Sustainability Victoria reports that 25-35% of a home's heat loss occurs through the roof, so insulation in your ceiling, walls, and floors can boost your home’s heat retention during winter. Keep your home insulated with:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>&gt; Ceiling insulation</strong>: Is your ceiling insulation up to standard? If it’s worn out or insufficient, install new insulation to prevent heat loss.<br/></li>
<li><strong>&gt; Wall insulation</strong>: If your walls are not insulated, add wall insulation. It’s especially important for older properties.<br/></li>
<li><strong>&gt; Floor insulation</strong>: Don’t forget about the floors. In colder climates, such as Melbourne or rural Victoria, the ground can draw heat away from your living space.<br/></li>
