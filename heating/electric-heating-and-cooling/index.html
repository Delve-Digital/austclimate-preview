A Step-by-Step Guide to Maintaining Your Electric Heating and Cooling System

Maintaining your electric heating and cooling system in top condition is key to its longevity, performance, and efficiency. The following are steps that you can take:
  • Regularly clean or replace the filters of your system to ensure efficient airflow and performance.
  • Keep the area around your heating and cooling system clear of debris, plants, and other items that could obstruct airflow.
  • Get your system serviced regularly by professionals like Australian Climate Systems to keep it running optimally.
The risk of breakdowns or malfunction is lower when you maintain your system properly. Regular maintenance can help select efficiencies that would otherwise go unnoticed and can lead to significant costly repairs or replacements. Rinnai Energy Saver

With a minimum energy efficiency rating of 4.8 Stars, our range of Energysaver® heaters outperform almost all other forms of heating. Sending out warm, clean air, they create zero emissions into the room, which is ideal for those susceptible to allergies or respiratory problems.
